## Reviewed concepts

### Research-only monetary-policy implementation

1. Federal funds target range vs effective federal funds rate.
2. IORB as an administered rate rather than a transaction-based market rate.
3. ON RRP as a rate-floor tool available to a broader eligible counterparty set than IORB.
4. SOFR vs EFFR as secured vs unsecured overnight funding benchmarks.

Primary sources: Federal Reserve Board and Federal Reserve Bank of New York.

### Open Treasury-market foundations

5. Fixed-rate Treasury prices and yields move in opposite directions.
6. Coupon rate is not the same object as yield to maturity.
7. Longer maturity generally means greater interest-rate sensitivity for otherwise similar fixed-rate bonds.
8. TIPS adjust principal with CPI while the stated coupon rate remains fixed.

Primary sources: U.S. Treasury / TreasuryDirect and SEC Investor.gov.
