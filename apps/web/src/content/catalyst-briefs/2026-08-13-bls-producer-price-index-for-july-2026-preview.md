### Quick pre-release note (publish-date: 2026-08-13 UTC)

- The BLS has scheduled the Producer Price Index for July 2026 on 2026-08-13 at 08:30 a.m. ET (embargoed until publication). See BLS and FRED calendars in the source ledger.

- Why it matters: PPI measures upstream price movements (final demand, intermediate demand, commodity inputs). Persistent strength in PPI components—especially core or intermediate-demand categories—can be interpreted as renewed pipeline inflation and may influence market-implied Fed policy expectations, Treasury yields, and the dollar.

- Market mechanics: watch front-end Treasuries and fed funds futures/CME FedWatch for immediate repricing; DXY and equity indices typically respond to directional surprises in real-time.
