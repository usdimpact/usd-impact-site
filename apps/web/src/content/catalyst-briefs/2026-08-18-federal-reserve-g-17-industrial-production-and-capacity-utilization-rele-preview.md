### What I verified (pre-event)

- Official Fed schedule: the Federal Reserve Board’s G.17 release schedule lists 2026-08-18 as a monthly G.17 publication date and states monthly releases are issued at 9:15 a.m. (the Board’s published G.17 materials and archived releases confirm the schedule and timing). See source ledger for the exact Fed release page and the current G.17 PDF.  

- Scope and timing: G.17 reports industrial production (output), industrial capacity, and capacity utilization for the U.S. industrial sector (manufacturing, mining, electric and gas utilities). Monthly G.17 releases report the prior month’s data (the series are published roughly on the mid-month schedule shown on the Board’s release calendar).  

- Annual revision note: Board materials indicate an annual revision to the IP/CU indexes is planned for autumn 2026; monitor that revision because it can alter historical comparators.

### How to use this ahead of the release (operationally concise)

1) Confirm the exact release timestamp on the Board’s G.17 landing page just before the event (the Board’s schedule is the primary timing confirmation).  
2) Assemble consensus numbers for the headline month-over-month IP and capacity utilization and the prior-month revisions (these are the most market-sensitive line items).  
3) Monitor the key subcomponents (manufacturing, motor vehicles, business equipment, petroleum & coal products) for asymmetric risks to the headline.  
4) Watch market reaction in the first 15–60 minutes for directional moves in DXY, 2‑yr / 10‑yr yields, and equity futures; those moves will reflect whether the print shifts growth or inflation expectations relative to priced policy odds.
