### Key outcome (verified)
- The BEA released "Personal Income and Outlays, July 2026" on 2026‑08‑26 confirming that the PCE price index rose 0.2% month‑over‑month and core PCE (ex food & energy) also rose 0.2% MoM; on a 12‑month basis headline PCE was +3.7% and core PCE +3.3%. (Sources: BEA news release; BEA PCE data page.)

### Conditional cross‑asset takeaways
- Dollar (DXY): hotter core PCE could strengthen the dollar via higher near‑term policy expectations; cooler prints could weaken the dollar.  
- U.S. rates: hotter core prints tend to lift short‑dated yields (revising in Fed path); cooler prints can lower/flatten the curve.  
- Equities: hot inflation raises discount‑rate pressure—negative for long‑duration growth/Nasdaq—while cooler inflation can relieve rate pressure but may also signal weaker demand.  
- Cross‑asset flows: sustained surprises change risk appetite and carry/FX funding dynamics (EM and credit sensitive to USD and rate moves).

- Intraday moves in 2y and 10y U.S. Treasury yields.  
- DXY and EUR/USD / USD/JPY immediate reaction.  
- Relative sector moves in S&P 500 vs Nasdaq.  
- Changes in breakeven inflation swaps (5y5y).  
- Upcoming CPI and payrolls releases and Fed speaker schedule that could confirm or offset the PCE signal.
