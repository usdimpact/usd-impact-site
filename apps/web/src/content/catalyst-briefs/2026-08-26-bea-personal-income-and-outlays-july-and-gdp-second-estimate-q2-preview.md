**Quick pre-event check (official timing confirmed)**

- What: BEA will publish Personal Income and Outlays (for July 2026, which contains the PCE price index) and the GDP second estimate (Q2 2026) on 2026-08-26. ([bea.gov](https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule/full))

- When (exact): 08:30 a.m. Eastern (EDT) on August 26, 2026 — that is 12:30 UTC. The BEA schedule and New York Fed economic calendar list the releases for that date and time. ([bea.gov](https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule/full)) ([newyorkfed.org](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/calendars/i-aug26.html))

**Confirmed baseline (what’s already known)**

- BEA’s advance estimate (July 30, 2026) showed Q2 real GDP +1.5% (annualized) and reported the PCE price index at an annualized +5.1% and core PCE at +3.4%. The upcoming second estimate will publish any revisions to those numbers and additional detail. ([bea.gov](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-2nd-quarter-2026))

- Personal Income and Outlays is the official BEA release for the PCE price index. The Federal Reserve’s longer-run inflation objective is 2% as measured by the annual change in the PCE price index, making the release relevant to policy expectations. ([federalreserve.gov](https://www.federalreserve.gov/economy-at-a-glance-inflation-pce.htm))

**How markets can react (conditional, non-prescriptive)**

- If core PCE prints materially above expectations while GDP is revised up, front-end yields and the dollar may move higher as market expectations for the policy path adjust; equities may face pressure if yields rise. Conversely, softer-than-expected PCE or weaker GDP revisions may ease rate pressure. Direction and magnitude remain conditional on the size and mix of the surprises and simultaneous news flows.

**Operational checklist for the release window**

- Watch the headline and core PCE month-over-month prints and calculate shorter-run annualized measures only after the official data are available.
- Monitor revisions in the GDP second estimate versus the advance estimate, including real GDP and relevant price indexes. ([bea.gov](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-2nd-quarter-2026))
- Track front-end Treasuries, the 2s/10s curve, DXY, and Fed funds futures through the immediate post-release window. ([newyorkfed.org](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/calendars/i-aug26.html))
