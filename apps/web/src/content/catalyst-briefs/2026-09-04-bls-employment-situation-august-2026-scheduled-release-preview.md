## Confirmed release timing

- BLS schedules the Employment Situation for August 2026 for Friday, September 4 at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time. The BLS calendar states that its listed release times are Eastern Time.
- The upcoming August figures are not available before the scheduled release. The current Employment Situation page still presents the July report and identifies the August report as the next release.

## Stronger benchmark context

- On August 28, BLS published a preliminary March 2026 benchmark revision of -79,000 jobs, or -0.1 percent, for total nonfarm employment.
- That preliminary estimate is context, not a revision to the monthly data now in use. BLS states that the final benchmark revision will be incorporated with the January 2027 Employment Situation release in February 2027.

## Confirmed facts versus conditional interpretation

- Confirmed: the release schedule, the household- and establishment-survey structure, the measures those surveys publish, and the preliminary benchmark methodology described above.
- Conditional: possible reactions in the dollar, rates, and risk assets. Those reactions depend on the full report, expectations, positioning, and simultaneous news; they are not forecasts.

## Release checklist

- Confirm the headline payroll change and prior-month revisions.
- Read the unemployment rate alongside participation and the employment-population ratio.
- Check average hourly earnings and average weekly hours.
- Review industry breadth, multiple jobholders, and part-time-for-economic-reasons measures.
- Use the statistical-significance and sampling notes before treating small monthly changes as meaningful.
