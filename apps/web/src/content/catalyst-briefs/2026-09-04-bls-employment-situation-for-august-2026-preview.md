## Pre-event brief (BLS Employment Situation for August 2026 — pre-release)

- Official timing: The BLS has scheduled the Employment Situation (reference month: August 2026) for Friday, 4 September 2026 at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time (ET). (BLS schedule and news-release pages confirm this.)

- What the release contains: The Employment Situation publishes establishment (payroll) and household survey measures: nonfarm payroll change, unemployment rate, labor force/participation, average weekly hours, average hourly earnings, and routine revisions. The BLS also noted a 2026 preliminary benchmark revision to establishment survey data (published Aug 28, 2026) that market participants should consider when interpreting monthly revisions. (See BLS release notes.)

- Market context and conditional read: Payrolls, the unemployment rate, participation, hours and wages together inform near-term growth and inflation expectations and therefore can influence USD, U.S. yields, equities and Fed‑funds pricing. Direction is conditional on the full data mix — headline payrolls alone can be misleading without wages, participation and revisions.

- Operational watch: Expect fast market moves around the 08:30 ET (12:30 UTC) print. Use BLS tables A (payrolls) and A‑1..A‑8 plus household tables on the CPS page to check the unemployment rate, participation, multiple jobholders, and wages. Verify whether the BLS posts any additional notes on revisions or benchmarking in the published release appendix.

Sources: Official BLS schedule and release pages (source ledger).
