### Event (concise)
Treasury announced on 2026-08-19 that it will increase the maximum size of liquidity-support buybacks for longer-dated nominal coupons (10Y–20Y and 20Y–30Y) from $2 billion to at least $4 billion per operation, effective 2026-09-09 through 2026-11-04.

### Official timing / mechanics (what the record shows)
- Treasury’s August 5 Q3 tentative schedule lists the 10Y–20Y operation with a September 9 announcement date, September 10 operation date, 1:40 p.m.–2:00 p.m. ET operation window, September 11 settlement, and a $2 billion maximum. The schedule also states that preliminary eligible CUSIPs are disclosed at 11:00 a.m. ET on the announcement date and the final list at 11:00 a.m. ET on the operation date.
- Treasury’s later August 19 announcement supersedes the long-end maximum effective September 9: the current $2 billion maximum becomes at least $4 billion per operation for 10Y–20Y and 20Y–30Y nominal sectors through November 4. Treasury said an updated tentative schedule would be released later.
- Treasury’s August 5 Quarterly Refunding statement confirms the quarter’s buyback program and says the next Quarterly Refunding announcement is scheduled for November 4, when Treasury will provide information about future buyback sizes.

### Market context / immediate reaction
- AP reported an immediate decline in 10- and 30-year Treasury yields and an initial lift in U.S. equities after the August 19 announcement. That observed reaction is consistent with a technical-liquidity transmission channel but does not establish a persistent macro effect.

### Practical notes for desks
- Treat the August 19 Treasury announcement as the controlling source for the higher long-end maximum effective September 9, while using the August 5 Q3 schedule for the planned announcement/operation/settlement dates and standard timing. Confirm the September 10 operation’s final eligible CUSIPs and maximum purchase amount from Treasury’s operation-day materials before relying on them.
