### Event (concise)
Treasury announced on 2026‑08‑19 that it will increase the maximum size of liquidity‑support buybacks for longer‑dated nominal coupons (10Y–20Y and 20Y–30Y) to at least $4bn per operation, effective 2026‑09‑09 and in effect through the current refunding quarter (through 2026‑11‑04). 

### Official timing / mechanics (what the record shows)
- Treasury’s tentative buyback schedule and explanatory notes show the procedural timings Treasury uses: preliminary eligible CUSIPs disclosed at 11:00 a.m. ET on the Announcement Date; operations are executed in a published window (typically 1:40 p.m. – 2:00 p.m. ET); settlement is the following business day. See the Tentative Schedule (For Publication 2026‑08‑05). 
- Treasury’s Quarterly Refunding statement (2026‑08‑05) released the tentative schedule for the refunding quarter and notes the next Quarterly Refunding will occur on 2026‑11‑04 when Treasury will provide information about future buyback sizes. 

### Market context / immediate reaction
- News reports showed an immediate market reaction: AP documented intra‑day falls in 10‑ and 30‑year yields and a modest equity lift after the announcement; Treasury framed the increase as intended to provide greater liquidity support in long‑dated nominal sectors. These reports are consistent with the technical‑liquidity transmission channel but do not quantify sustained macro effects. 

### Practical notes for desks
- Expect the operational timeline to be: Announcement Date (prelim CUSIPs at 11:00 a.m. ET) → Operation Date (execution window ~1:40–2:00 p.m. ET) → Settlement the following business day. Confirm each buyback’s Announcement/Operation dates and CUSIPs on treasury.gov when Treasury posts the updated tentative schedule and the specific announcement for the operation.
