## Executive pre-event brief

### Confirmed timing and items

- The FOMC is scheduled for September 15–16, 2026. The Federal Reserve calendar lists the meeting release at 2:00 p.m. ET on Wednesday, September 16, followed by the Chair’s press conference at 2:30 p.m. ET.
- The September meeting is marked as one associated with the Summary of Economic Projections (SEP).
- The BLS release calendar scheduled the August 2026 CPI for September 11 at 8:30 a.m. ET. This preview does not state the CPI result without a separately verified outcome source.

### What this means

- The most consequential communication channels are the policy statement, the SEP and dot plot, and the Chair’s press conference. Changes in expected policy path can transmit quickly through Treasury yields, the U.S. dollar, and risk assets.

### What to watch in practice

- Compare the statement language with the July 2026 statement, focusing on inflation, labor-market conditions, the balance of risks, and data dependence.
- Review the SEP and dot plot for changes in the projected federal-funds-rate path and the distribution of participant projections.
- During the press conference, distinguish confirmed policy decisions from conditional guidance and answers that depend on future data.
- Treat immediate market moves as reactions to new information and positioning, not as proof of a durable trend.

## Key takeaway

The September 15–16 FOMC meeting is a scheduled SEP meeting with the statement listed for 2:00 p.m. ET and the Chair’s press conference for 2:30 p.m. ET on September 16. The practical task is to compare the new policy language and projections with prior guidance while separating verified facts from market interpretation.
