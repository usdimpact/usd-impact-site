- Markets price expectations using Fed funds futures/CME FedWatch; monitor the live FedWatch probabilities immediately before 14:00 ET and again after the 14:30 ET press conference for potential sharp repricing in U.S. rates, USD and risk assets.

- What to monitor in the statement and press conference: directional cues on the path of rates, any change to balance‑sheet language, and whether the Chair signals a conditional (data‑dependent) path or stronger commitment to a particular rate path. The exact timing and convention above are confirmed by the Federal Reserve calendar and press‑release guidance.
