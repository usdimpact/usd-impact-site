## Summary
Primary-source material from the Board (Implementation Note, FOMC statement) and the Board's data feed confirm the operational implementation described in the candidate event: IORB was set to 3.90% effective 2026-09-17 and the FOMC target range is 3.75–4.00%. The Implementation Note also specifies Desk operating parameters (standing repo and ON RRP settings). See source ledger for links.

## Confirmed facts (separate from conditional interpretation)
- IORB raised to 3.90% effective 2026-09-17 (Board Implementation Note; Policy Rates DDP).  
- FOMC set the federal funds target range to 3.75–4.00% (FOMC statement).  
- The Implementation Note instructs the Open Market Desk to operate with standing‑repo and ON‑RRP parameters (standing repo at 4.0%; ON RRP offering 3.75% with $160bn per‑counterparty limit) consistent with the target range.

## Conditional cross‑asset transmission (interpretation)
- Overnight money markets: the direct mechanism is immediate — IORB and ON RRP set a corridor and therefore tend to re‑anchor overnight rates, but the magnitude and sign of spread moves depend on ON RRP take‑up and standing‑repo demand.  
- Short‑end Treasury yields: higher short‑term policy rates and more expensive standing repo could lift bill yields, conditional on issuance and dealer balance‑sheet capacity.  
- FX: clearer overnight policy settings may support the USD, although longer‑dated rate expectations and risk sentiment can offset that effect.  
- Equities: higher short‑term rates and signals of policy persistence could weigh on risk assets through discount‑rate and funding channels; near‑term moves will reflect positioning and macro updates.

## Recommended immediate checks (what to watch)
1) ON RRP results (volume and counterparties) for 2026-09-17 and the next 3 days.  
2) Standing repo operation results and any Desk announcements.  
3) Effective federal funds and H.15 short‑maturity yields on 2026-09-17/18.  
4) T‑bill auction coverage/tails in the coming week.  
5) USD index (DXY) and short‑dated swap/forward points over 48 hours.  
6) Intraday S&P 500/Nasdaq moves and VIX.
