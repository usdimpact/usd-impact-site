## USD Impact Catalyst — Pre‑Event Brief (BEA advance estimate, 3Q 2026) — 2026-10-29 UTC

- Official timing (confirmed): BEA will release the GDP (Advance Estimate), 3rd Quarter 2026 on October 29, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) — that is 12:30 UTC. BEA’s journalist guidance and release schedule list the embargoed release time at 8:30 a.m. EDT.

- What the advance estimate contains (why watch): the advance vintage is the first official quarterly GDP reading and reports headline real GDP (SAAR), current‑dollar GDP, real final sales to private domestic purchasers, and BEA price measures including the PCE price index and core PCE. BEA also provides technical notes and source‑data that identify which components (inventories, investment, consumption, government, trade) drove the quarter.

- Market relevance (concise): the advance estimate is a major growth datapoint that can shift near‑term Fed and yield expectations. Headline upside surprises tend to support USD and push yields higher; downside surprises can relieve tightening concerns and favor risk‑on/softening‑USD outcomes — but component detail (e.g., strength concentrated in inventories vs. broad final demand) matters for durability and market response.

- Practical pre‑release checklist for traders/analysts: confirm the embargo lift at 08:30 a.m. EDT / 12:30 UTC; prepare to observe headline GDP vs consensus, PCE and core PCE movements, and the contributions table (inventories, investment, trade). Monitor initial order flow in Treasuries, USD (DXY), and equity futures in the first 30–60 minutes for directional confirmation.
