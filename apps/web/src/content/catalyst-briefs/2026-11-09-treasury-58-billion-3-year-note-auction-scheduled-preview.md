### Quick pre‑event brief — Treasury $58bn 3‑year note auction (preview)

What: U.S. Treasury will offer $58 billion of 3‑year notes as part of its November quarterly refunding. The sale was announced by Treasury in its quarterly refunding statement. See Treasury quarterly refunding documents and data pages in the source ledger.

When: Auction scheduled for Monday, 2026‑11‑09 at 1:00 p.m. ET (yield basis). Settlement is scheduled for Monday, 2026‑11‑16. (Official schedule and settlement details: Treasury quarterly refunding.)

Why it matters: Auction demand, bid composition (direct/indirect/dealer) and the accepted high yield are short‑term drivers for USD‑rates liquidity and can produce knock‑on effects in cash/repo and futures markets and, to a lesser extent, in FX (DXY).

Watchlist (top priorities on auction day): bid‑to‑cover and investor class splits; stop‑out (accepted) yield vs. pre‑auction curve; dealer appetite and allotments; immediate reactions in cash/repo/futures; short‑term DXY/FX moves.

Primary sources for timing and size are the Treasury Quarterly Refunding statement and the Treasury data pages in the source ledger. Market context and positioning ahead of the refunding are provided by market reporting (see Bloomberg).
