## Pre‑event briefing (publication‑ready)

Confirmed facts

- The U.S. Department of the Treasury announced on 2026-11-04 that it will offer a 3‑year note of $58 billion (maturing 2029‑11‑15). The auction is scheduled for 1:00 p.m. ET on Monday, 2026-11-09 with settlement on Monday, 2026-11-16. (Treasury Quarterly Refunding Statement).  
- The auction schedule and supporting refunding documents were published in Treasury’s Nov 2–Nov 4, 2026 Quarterly Refunding release (auction schedule available in the published XML/PDF). (Treasury Quarterly Refunding Documents).  
- Market calendars note the 3‑year monthly offering has been $58 billion in recent quarters (contextual reference). (CME Group).

Conditional interpretation (what this may transmit to markets)

- The auction’s immediate market impact will be driven first by the stop‑out (accepted) yield and the bid‑to‑cover/indirect‑bidder share. A weak stop‑out or low coverage tends to lift short‑term Treasury yields and can prompt dollar support via safe‑haven and rate‑expectation channels.  
- Dealer willingness to absorb the allocation and the surrounding cash/TGA/Fed operations backdrop will condition how quickly the supply is soaked up; thin dealer balance sheets or tight cash could amplify price moves.  
- Typical transmission timing: auction is executed at 13:00 ET; the most actionable market moves frequently occur within the auction window and the following 10–60 minutes as primary dealers and indirect bidders trade in the secondary market.

What watchers should prepare

- Monitor live auction prints (stop‑out yield, bid‑to‑cover, allocation to indirect bidders) at 13:00 ET on 2026-11-09.  
- Compare accepted yield to pre‑auction secondary 3‑year yields and watch immediate on‑the‑run/off‑the‑run basis moves.  
- Watch USD (DXY) reaction and cash market liquidity in the 0–60 minute window after the auction; check dealer inventories and interdealer spreads if available.
