Event: Treasury $58bn 3‑year note auction — preview (scheduled)

Key points

- The U.S. Treasury’s official quarterly refunding statement (Nov 4, 2026) confirms a $58 billion 3‑year note auction to be held at 1:00 p.m. ET on Monday, November 9, 2026, with settlement Monday, November 16, 2026. (Sources: Treasury press release.)
- The 3‑year is one leg of a $125 billion November refunding (3‑, 10‑ and 30‑year coupons). TreasuryDirect’s auction cadence guidance and market calendars are consistent with the announced timing. (Sources: Treasury press release; TreasuryDirect; CME Group calendar.)

What to watch for during the auction

- Stop‑out (accepted) yield relative to pre‑auction levels and recent on‑the‑run 3‑year prints.
- Bid‑to‑cover and the fraction allocated to indirect bidders vs. primary dealers.
- Primary dealer take and subsequent dealer repo/funding behavior into settlement (Nov 16, 2026).
- Intraday repricing in nearby cash yields (2y–5y), 3‑year futures, and any USD (DXY) response.

Conditional interpretation (not a verified fact)

- A materially weaker bid (low bid‑to‑cover and large dealer take) could tend to push short‑end yields higher and tighten dealer balance sheets, which may transiently pressure term funding costs and influence USD direction. Conversely, strong indirect demand could be associated with stable or lower stop‑out yields.
