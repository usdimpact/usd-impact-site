## title: "BTCUSD" slug: "/glossary/btcusd" status: "ready-for-build" doNotTranslate: false definition: "The Bitcoin price expressed in U.S. dollars. Bitcoin is a fixed-supply digital asset that has historically shown sensitivity to global liquidity conditions and risk appetite. Its relationships with dollar direction and macro variables are less established than gold, can be highly volatile, and are regime-dependent."
