---
title: "Risk-Off"
slug: "/glossary/risk-off"
status: "ready-for-build"
doNotTranslate: false
definition: "A market environment in which investors reduce exposure to perceived riskier assets — such as equities, high-yield bonds, emerging markets, and Bitcoin — and move toward perceived safe havens such as U.S. Treasuries, the dollar, gold, Swiss franc, or Japanese yen. Risk-off episodes are often triggered by economic uncertainty, geopolitical stress, or tightening liquidity conditions."
