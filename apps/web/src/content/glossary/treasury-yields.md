definition: "The interest rates on U.S. government bonds, ranging from short-term bills (3 months) to long-term bonds (30 years). The 10-year Treasury yield is a key global benchmark for borrowing costs. Rising yields historically strengthen the dollar by attracting capital flows into U.S. assets. Published by the U.S. Treasury and available on FRED."
