definition: "The CBOE Volatility Index. Measures expected 30-day volatility in the S&P 500, derived from options pricing. Often called the fear gauge. A rising VIX typically signals risk-off conditions and increasing market stress; a falling VIX signals calmer markets and higher risk appetite. Published by the CBOE. Not a predictive signal on its own."
