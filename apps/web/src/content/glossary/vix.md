definition: "The CBOE Volatility Index. Measures expected 30-day volatility in the S&P 500 derived from options pricing. Often called the fear gauge. A rising VIX typically signals risk-off conditions and increasing market stress. Published by the CBOE. Used as one input among several — not a predictive signal on its own."
