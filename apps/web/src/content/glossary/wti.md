definition: "West Texas Intermediate. The primary U.S. crude oil benchmark, priced in U.S. dollars per barrel. Because oil is globally priced in dollars, dollar strength can historically pressure WTI prices by making oil more expensive for non-dollar buyers. Data published by the EIA and CME Group."
