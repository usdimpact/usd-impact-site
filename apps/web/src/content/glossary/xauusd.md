## title: "XAUUSD" slug: "/glossary/xauusd" status: "ready-for-build" doNotTranslate: false definition: "The spot gold price expressed in U.S. dollars per troy ounce. Gold is one of the most dollar-sensitive assets: it historically tends to respond to changes in real rates and dollar direction. Often monitored as a monetary reserve asset and inflation hedge, though relationships with the dollar are regime-dependent and not guaranteed."
