Conditional cross‑asset interpretation (education, not a forecast)
- Lower oil prices could reduce near‑term headline inflation risk and put downward pressure on Treasury yields, all else equal. Lower yields can support long-duration equities and gold through the discount-rate channel, while stronger risk appetite can work in the opposite direction for defensive gold demand.
- These relationships are conditional and can be offset by new geopolitical shocks, stronger macro data or shifts in Fed communication.

Risks / what could change the picture
- Renewed escalation in the Middle East that reverses the oil reprieve. This could raise oil and inflation risk premia; the Treasury and dollar response would depend on the balance between inflation, growth and safe-haven effects.
- Stronger than expected U.S. labor or inflation prints (JOLTS, payrolls, CPI in the coming week) that reinforce persistence of elevated inflation and keep rates higher for longer.
