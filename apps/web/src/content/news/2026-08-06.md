  - The Treasury’s tentative six‑month auction schedule lists multiple bill and note auctions across Aug 6–13, 2026 including short‑dated bills and coupon reopenings/new issues in the 3y/10y/30y sectors. Source: treasury-auction-schedule and treasurydirect-schedule.
- Conditional interpretation (educational language):

## Risks / what would change this view
- If primary dealer demand at the 10y/30y auctions is stronger-than-expected, yields could fall (reducing upward pressure on USD) instead of rising. Conversely, weak demand or heavy competitive dealer selling could amplify yield moves.
- Unexpected Fed communication, off‑cycle liquidity operations, large private-sector flows, or an exogenous geopolitical shock in the 36‑hour window would change the priority drivers.
