Conditional cross-asset implications
- If payrolls on Sep 4 exceed expectations and unemployment falls, market pricing for tighter Fed policy could increase, which tends to strengthen the USD and lift U.S. rates while pressuring risk assets. Conversely, a softer print could reduce rate-tightening odds, which may weigh on the dollar and support equities.

- Incoming PCE data consistent with elevated inflation would support persistent rate premium priced into U.S. rates and the dollar; disinflationary prints would work in the opposite direction.
