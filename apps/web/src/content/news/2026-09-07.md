Risks and conditional market implications
- Stronger incoming labor, growth, or inflation data could sustain upward pressure on short-term Treasury yields and the dollar, weighing on rate-sensitive equities. Softer data would tend to ease some near-term tightening expectations.
- Treasury’s September 9 buyback change is a technical liquidity event rather than a monetary-policy action; its cross-asset effects depend on dealer positioning, long-end demand, and broader risk conditions.

Watchlist (near-term)
- Monitor follow-through in short-end Treasury yields and DXY as markets continue to absorb the August employment and Q2 growth data.
- Watch the September 9 Treasury long-end buyback change for effects on long-end technical conditions, curve positioning, and USD funding sensitivity.
