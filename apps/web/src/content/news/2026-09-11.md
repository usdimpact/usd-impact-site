## Executive view
Energy-driven inflation risk dominated recent market attention after the EIA raised its Brent outlook for 2H26 and flagged low distillate inventories—factors that increase the probability of upside shocks to CPI ahead of the Federal Reserve’s Sept 15–16 FOMC meeting. The immediate data trigger is the BLS CPI release for August on Sept 11; the FOMC meeting two weeks later remains the central policy event.

## Key drivers (verified)
- EIA STEO (September): Raised Brent price projection for 2H26 and forecast U.S. distillate inventories to fall below five-year lows in September and stay low through late 2026–27. This increases upside oil-price risk that can feed into headline CPI. (Source: EIA STEO).
- BLS schedule: Confirms the August CPI release at 08:30 ET on Sept 11, the last major monthly inflation read before the Sept 15–16 FOMC meeting. (Source: BLS schedule).
- Federal Reserve calendar: Confirms the FOMC meeting and press conference on Sept 15–16 as the primary policy event for markets. (Source: Federal Reserve calendar).
- World Gold Council commentary: Gold demand and flows show elevated interest amid volatile macro conditions and central-bank uncertainty. (Source: World Gold Council Sep posts).

## Catalysts & near-term watch
- Sept 11, 2026 — BLS CPI (Aug) at 08:30 ET: immediate market trigger for Fed expectations and U.S. yields. (Source: BLS schedule).
- Sept 15–16, 2026 — FOMC meeting & press conference: primary central-bank event that will set policy guidance and influence term premia and DXY. (Source: Federal Reserve calendar).

## Risks
- Oil supply shocks or faster-than-expected inventory draws could lift headline CPI and force a reassessment of Fed tightening odds.
- Market positioning ahead of Sept 11 CPI and Sept 15–16 FOMC could amplify moves in rates and equities if surprises occur.

## Watchlist (next 7 days)
- Aug CPI (BLS, Sep 11) print and components (energy, shelter) for direction on headline and core inflation.
- FOMC communications Sept 15–16 and any shifts in forward guidance.
- Oil inventory prints and EIA weekly reports for continuation of the near-term oil trend.
