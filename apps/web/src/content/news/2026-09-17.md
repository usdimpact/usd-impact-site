Executive view

The Federal Open Market Committee raised the federal funds target range on 2026-09-16 and the Board published implementation instructions that raise the interest paid on reserve balances effective 2026-09-17. Short-term rate markets and interest-rate futures reacted sharply, lifting front-end yields and volatility. These developments tighten dollar funding and support a firmer DXY, while increasing effective short-term borrowing costs for banks and other dollar-market participants.
