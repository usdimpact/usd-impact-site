## Why it matters

The absence of a fresh official data print does not imply unchanged markets. Bank credit and household finances can help explain conditions affecting policy and dollar demand, but their observation windows are distinct.


## What moves it

Policy-rate expectations, inflation compensation, real yields, currency funding and changes in bank balance sheets can influence the dollar transmission chain. These drivers are not interchangeable and each needs observations from a comparable period. Energy also reflects physical supply and demand, while equities depend on earnings and valuation.

## Common mistake

Treating a release date as the observation date; presenting an older, holiday-affected reading as a fresh intraday price; or using nominal yields as a substitute for the ten-year TIPS real yield.

## Key takeaway

Keep release dates, survey years, observation periods and future-event status separate. Without verified current USD, real-yield and stress indicators, no directional trade conclusion is justified.
