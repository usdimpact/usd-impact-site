## What moves it

Use the dollar-transmission framework as a set of conditional questions, not as a claim about Friday's prices. Does new information change expected monetary policy? Do observed real yields move in the same direction as nominal yields? Is the dollar responding to relative growth, rates or funding demand? Are broader risk conditions reinforcing or offsetting that response?

For **WTI**, separate the inflation discussion from independently verified physical oil-market evidence. For **Henry Hub, TTF and LNG**, avoid substituting U.S. household gas-service inflation for wholesale prices, storage, weather or export-flow evidence. For **XAUUSD and BTCUSD**, assess the dollar, real yields and liquidity independently rather than assume a uniform reaction. For the **S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow Jones and Mag 7**, also consider company-specific earnings evidence. No direction for these assets is assigned in this edition.

## Common mistake

Treating yesterday's PPI as today's release; labeling a scheduled event as a published result; confusing PPI energy, CPI gasoline and wholesale futures returns; or reading a green publication status as proof that the unattended generator recovered. These are different observations and require different evidence.
