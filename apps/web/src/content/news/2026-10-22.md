## What the evidence means

**Purchasing power:** A rise in nominal median pay is not the same as an improvement in real purchasing power. Keep the population definition, seasonal treatment and comparison period intact. Do not convert a quarterly worker-sample result into a claim about all households or current monthly wage momentum.

**Regional reports:** Treat the Beige Book as evidence from business and community contacts with a stated collection cutoff. It adds context; it does not settle the next policy decision.

**Policy calendar:** The upcoming FOMC entry is retained as a structured, source-backed catalyst. A meeting schedule is not an announcement of what policymakers will do.
