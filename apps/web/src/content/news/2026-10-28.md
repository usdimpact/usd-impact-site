## Executive view

Fresh BLS regional labor data arrived before today's scheduled Federal Reserve decision. BLS reported that September unemployment rates were lower than a year earlier in 226 of 387 metropolitan areas, higher in 138 and unchanged in 23. Nonfarm employment increased over the year in 11 metropolitan areas, decreased in 3 and was essentially unchanged in 373.

The Federal Reserve calendar places the October 27-28 FOMC communication at 2:00 p.m. U.S. Eastern, followed by the press conference at 2:30 p.m. This edition is timestamped before that scheduled communication and therefore treats it as a catalyst rather than an outcome.

The next macro window is also unusually concentrated. BEA identifies October 29 as the next release date for both the advance estimate of third-quarter GDP and September Personal Income and Outlays, including PCE inflation data. BLS lists the third-quarter Employment Cost Index for October 30.

## What changed today

**Regional labor data:** The October 28 BLS release shows that year-over-year unemployment rates fell in most metropolitan areas, but the employment picture was much less uniform. Only 11 metropolitan areas recorded statistically significant year-over-year nonfarm employment increases, 3 recorded decreases and 373 were essentially unchanged.

That combination is useful as a regional labor signal, but it should not be converted into a direct call on the dollar, rates or equities. The release is regional, not a replacement for the national Employment Situation report.

## Policy window

**FOMC:** The Federal Reserve calendar confirms the two-day October 27-28 meeting and schedules the meeting communication for 2:00 p.m. U.S. Eastern, followed by the press conference at 2:30 p.m.

Because this edition precedes those scheduled communications, it does not state a policy decision, implementation detail or market reaction. Those items require verification from the official Federal Reserve release after publication.

## What comes next

**October 29:** BEA is scheduled to publish the advance estimate of third-quarter 2026 GDP and September Personal Income and Outlays. The latter includes the PCE and core PCE price indexes.

**October 30:** BLS is scheduled to publish the third-quarter Employment Cost Index at 8:30 a.m. U.S. Eastern.

The sequence matters because policy interpretation can change quickly when an FOMC decision is followed immediately by new growth, inflation and labor-cost evidence.

## Three Dials: what remains unverified here

The cited official documents establish today's regional labor release and the near-term policy/data calendar. They do not establish a current direction for the broad dollar, 10-year TIPS real yields or liquidity/stress conditions.

This edition therefore avoids assigning a bullish or bearish cross-asset regime before those market variables are verified independently.

## What to watch next

1. Verify the Federal Reserve's October 28 statement and implementation details from the official release after 2:00 p.m. U.S. Eastern.
2. Separate the policy decision from the 2:30 p.m. press conference; statement language and press-conference guidance can affect expectations differently.
