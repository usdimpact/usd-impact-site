## Executive view

The principal October 29 developments are **official BEA releases**, not a forecast. Third-quarter U.S. real GDP expanded at a **3.4% annualized rate** in the advance estimate, compared with **2.2% in Q2**. September headline PCE inflation reached **0.5% month over month** and **3.6% year over year**. Core PCE inflation, excluding food and energy, was **0.3% monthly** and **3.1% annually**.

These data arrived after the **October 28 Federal Reserve decision** to maintain its target range at **3.75%–4.00%**, approved by a **10–2 vote**. The reports describe a growth-and-inflation mix, not a verified same-day USD or asset-price move. The primary-source ledger separates the October 29 data releases from the earlier Fed decision and the upcoming scheduled labor-cost release.

## What changed

**Growth — BEA GDP:** The Q3 advance estimate of real GDP is **3.4% at a seasonally adjusted annual rate**, after **2.2%** in Q2. BEA identifies stronger investment, consumer spending and government spending as contributors, partially offset by lower exports; imports also increased.

**Inflation — BEA PCE:** The September PCE **price index** rose **0.5% m/m, 3.6% y/y**; the measure **excluding food and energy** rose **0.3% m/m, 3.1% y/y**. BEA separately reports **nominal PCE spending** grew **0.5% on the month**. Do not confuse nominal spending growth with the PCE price index. Nor should quarterly annualized GDP-report price growth be interchanged with monthly or 12-month PCE inflation.

**Policy — Fed FOMC:** On October 28 the Committee held **3.75%–4.00%**. Lisa D. Cook and Beth M. Hammack favored a **25 bp increase**. The Fed's implementation note maintained IORB at **3.90%**, effective October 29. This is policy evidence, not evidence of a realized market reaction on October 29.

## Common mistake

Treating an official GDP or PCE surprise as proof that the dollar, yields or equities moved in a particular direction. The official release establishes the economic number; a market reaction needs separate, time-aligned market evidence.
