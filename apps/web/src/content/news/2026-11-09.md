Carry-forward edition: primary official releases within the prior 14 days anchor this review. - Treasury supply: the announced $125bn gross auctions refinance approximately $97bn maturing debt and raise approximately $28bn new cash. Demand and pricing at each auction could move yields and influence USD funding and risk-asset flows.
- Energy data timing: the WPSR release (Nov 12) will deliver fresh inventory and flow reads relevant for WTI/Brent and product markets.
- Labor backdrop: October payrolls and the 4.1% unemployment rate (BLS, Nov 6) remain relevant to Fed-rate expectations even though the release predates this edition.
