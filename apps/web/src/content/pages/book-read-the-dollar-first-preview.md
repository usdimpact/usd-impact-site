## 1. Read a real lesson

Start with **[Read the Dollar Before You Read the Asset](/start-here/)**.

The lesson demonstrates the core method used throughout USD Impact:

- distinguish the U.S. dollar from DXY;
- read **three macro dials** — dollar direction, real-rate pressure and liquidity stress;
- use broad-dollar and cross-asset evidence as confirmation rather than as additional dials;
- separate correlation from a plausible transmission mechanism;
- compare a rate-led strong-dollar regime with a stress-led strong-dollar regime; and
- keep conditional evidence separate from a forecast or trading instruction.

It is intentionally public. You do not need an account, purchase or entitlement to read it.

## 2. Take a real book checkpoint

After the lesson, take **[Start Here: Why the Dollar Moves Global Markets](/start-here/quiz/)**.

This is the live **Quiz 1 of 12** checkpoint used by the USD Impact learning system. It contains ten questions and normally takes about six to eight minutes.

The quiz tests whether you can:

- explain why the dollar matters beyond the FX market;
- distinguish DXY from the broader dollar system;
- separate FX depreciation from domestic purchasing-power loss;
- recognise a dollar-liquidity stress mechanism;
- use Broad USD as confirmation rather than assuming DXY tells the whole story;
- avoid automatic “USD up = asset down” rules; and
- apply the sequence **benchmark → driver → confirmation → cautious interpretation**.

You receive explanations and can retry without penalty. The public checkpoint lets you judge the level, language and reasoning style before deciding whether the full Guided Interactive Edition is useful to you.

## A useful way to judge the sample

Do not judge the material by whether it gives you a market call. That is not the product.

Instead, ask:

- Did the lesson make a confusing dollar concept easier to define?
- Did the worked examples help distinguish two regimes that can produce the same DXY direction?
- Did the quiz test reasoning rather than memorisation alone?
- Could you repeat the framework without relying on a prediction?
- Does the film syllabus cover the areas you want to understand in more depth?

If the answer is yes, the full edition extends that learning process. If not, the public lesson, quiz and catalog remain available without purchase.
