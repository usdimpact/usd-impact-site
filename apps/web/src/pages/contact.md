# Contact USD Impact

For product access, account, billing, privacy, accessibility, or general questions, email [support@usd-impact.com](mailto:support@usd-impact.com).

USD Impact is operated by **KELA LEADS S.R.L.**, Romania. Company and verification details are available on [About & transparency](/about/).

## Help us resolve your request

Please include the page or product involved and a short description of what happened. For accessibility barriers, tell us which page, format, or control caused difficulty and what assistive technology or browser you were using, if you are comfortable sharing that information.

Do not email passwords, passkey details, sign-in codes, full payment-card numbers, private keys, or other secrets. USD Impact support will not ask you to send them.

For policy details, see [Privacy](/privacy/), [Terms](/terms/), [Refund Policy](/refund-policy/), [Accessibility](/accessibility/), and [Compliance](/compliance/).
