# Privacy notice

_Last updated: July 20, 2026_

## What we collect

When you join the *Read the Dollar First* waitlist, we collect the email address you submit and the time the contact record is created.

## Why we collect it

We use this information only to:

- confirm that you joined the waitlist;
- send the purchase link when the book becomes available; and
- send essential availability updates directly related to the book.

Joining the waitlist does not subscribe you to unrelated market commentary, trading alerts, or third-party promotions.

## Service providers

The website is deployed through Vercel. Waitlist contacts and confirmation emails are processed through Resend. These providers process data on behalf of USD Impact to operate the waitlist and deliver email.

## Retention and deletion

Your waitlist record is retained until you unsubscribe, request deletion, or the waitlist is retired. You may withdraw at any time by replying to a waitlist email with a deletion request. Future availability messages will include an unsubscribe mechanism where required.

## Sharing and sale

USD Impact does not sell waitlist email addresses. Information may be shared only with service providers required to operate the website, store the waitlist, and deliver the requested email.

## Security

API credentials are stored as protected deployment environment variables and are not exposed in the public website source. No online system can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited to the services required to operate the waitlist.

## Your choice

Submitting the form is optional. The consent checkbox is not preselected. You may continue using the educational website without joining the waitlist.

This notice describes the book-waitlist workflow only and is not legal advice.
