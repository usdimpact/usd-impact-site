# USD Impact Benchmark Dashboard

## Positioning

A weekly educational framework for reading dollar-regime conditions across macro and market indicators.

## Boundary

Educational benchmark - not a trading signal.

## Phases

1. Static educational placeholder.
2. Manual weekly benchmark update.
3. Semi-automated data table with human approval.
4. Automated dashboard only after licensing, QA, uptime, and compliance controls.

## Modules

- Dollar Direction
- Rates and Real Yield Pressure
- Liquidity and Stress
- Asset Impact Layer
- Weekly Checklist
