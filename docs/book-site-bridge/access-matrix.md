## Rules

1. The bridge may explain a protected asset but may not expose or bypass it.
2. Public framework dependencies required to understand the book must not be moved behind an entitlement gate during this phase.
3. The Research Membership must not be required to understand or apply the book's core educational sequence.
4. No Phase 1 form transmits or stores an answer, financial profile, holding, objective or suitability fact.
5. Checkout, price, purchase state, entitlement state and protected media routes are outside this change.

## Release dependency

A later public release requires a separate approval covering sitemap/navigation treatment, public route naming, analytics, and any access-level changes. The present matrix documents intended boundaries; it does not mutate them.
