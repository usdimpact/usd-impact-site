# Contextual book and tool promotion policy

Status: required for every Book-Site Bridge placement.

## Principle

A link is allowed only when it helps the reader complete the current analytical task. The bridge must never convert education into repetitive product promotion.

## Book to site

A chapter may present one primary online practice destination when the tool directly implements the chapter's method.

The placement must state:

- what question the tool answers;
- the three-step use sequence;
- the chapter-specific reason for the link;
- the tool's limitation;
- the stable `/go/` alias.

Do not place general product advertising inside the chapter body.

## Site to book

A site tool may point to the book only when the chapter resolves a specific conceptual need, such as:

- DXY and broad USD divergence -> Chapter 3;
- unclear transmission channel -> Chapter 4;
- physical oil balance ignored -> Chapter 5;
- real yields underweighted in gold -> Chapter 6;
- short-run liquidity confused with the Bitcoin thesis -> Chapter 7;
- regional gas structure ignored -> Chapter 8;
- pair-specific FX move mistaken for a global regime -> Chapter 9;
- score limitations or historical evidence misunderstood -> Chapter 10;
- weekly sequence or dial order unclear -> Chapter 11;
- one-variable reasoning detected -> Chapter 12;
- current evidence not refreshed -> Chapter 13.

The link text must explain why the chapter is relevant. Generic `Buy the book` language is not sufficient.

## Frequency and interface rules

- one primary chapter connection per tool or article;
- no modal, interstitial, countdown or scarcity treatment;
- no profit, performance or outcome implication;
- no repeated promotion after dismissal in a future stateful implementation;
- no checkout link while public checkout is held;
- no implication that the book or tool is required for investment success;
- no use of the Score as a predictive sales claim.

## Approved language pattern

`Chapter 3 explains why this comparison uses both DXY and broad USD. Review it when the two measures diverge.`

## Disallowed language pattern

`Unlock the secret indicator and buy the book before the next move.`
