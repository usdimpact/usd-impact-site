### Terminology and risk reminders

- `DXY` is a *defined weighted currency index*, not the entire broad trade-weighted USD measure.
- `rendimientos reales`, `prima por plazo`, `diferenciales de crédito`, `haircut/recorte de valoración`, `repo`, `base entre divisas`, `PCE`, `GNL` and `TGA` have specific distinct technical meanings. Keep acronyms when useful.
- `funding stress` is financing-market pressure; do not translate it as proven capital shortage or as a trading signal.
- `swap lines` vs `FIMA repo`, `banca corresponsal`, `reservas bancarias` vs `depósitos` and cash vs collateral are **not** synonyms.
