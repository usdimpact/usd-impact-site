## Controlled distinction: hurdle rate

Historical Spanish Edition 1.2 used `tasa de descuento` for English `hurdle rate`.

That term is retired for Edition 1.3 because a discount rate is not the same concept.

Construction rule:
- narrative default: `rentabilidad mínima exigida`;
- glossary/formal definition: `tasa de rentabilidad mínima exigida`;
- apply consistently in Chapters 7, 11, 13, Appendix A, tables, captions and later audio scripts.

## Controlled distinction: real yields

Edition 1.2 often uses `tasas reales`. For Edition 1.3, use `rendimientos reales` when Candidate 2 refers to market yields, especially Treasury/TIPS yields. Preserve `tasa real` only when the source truly refers to a rate rather than a yield.

## Compliance phrase lock

Where Candidate 2 requires the complete chapter compliance boundary, the Spanish construction must preserve all meanings:

- material educativo e informativo;
- no constituye asesoramiento personalizado de inversión, legal, fiscal ni de trading;
- no es una señal de trading;
- no es una recomendación para comprar o vender valores, materias primas, divisas o activos digitales;
- las relaciones de mercado son condicionales;
- verifique los datos actuales antes de utilizarlos.

Exact punctuation may be normalized at final copy-edit; semantic exclusions may not be weakened.
