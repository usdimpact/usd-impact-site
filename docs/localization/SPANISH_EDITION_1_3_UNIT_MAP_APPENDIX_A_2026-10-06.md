## Hard retirement: historical Score glossary entry

The historical `USD Impact Score` glossary unit must not survive into Edition 1.3 because it states:

- moving-standard-deviation normalization;
- approximate three-standard-deviation clipping;
- aggregate 84.5% hit rate;
- 100 / 79.7 / 73.2 regime claims;
- fixed Friday 22:00 UTC recalculation wording.

The replacement methodology authority lives in Chapter 10 and Appendix B, not in this obsolete glossary block.

## Terminology decision required

English `hurdle rate` must not remain `tasa de descuento`.

The final Spanish term should clearly mean a minimum required/threshold return versus the next-best alternative. Once selected, apply it consistently to:

- Appendix A;
- Chapter 7;
- Chapter 11;
- Chapter 13;
- any captions, tables, audiobook script or companion lesson using the concept.
