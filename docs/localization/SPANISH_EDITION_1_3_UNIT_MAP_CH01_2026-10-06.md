## Unit model

Stable source IDs use `ES13-C01-Uxxx`.

Allowed states:

- `REUSE_VERIFIED` — historical Spanish unit has been freshly compared to Candidate 2 and preserves current semantic/compliance meaning.
- `REVISE` — historical Spanish unit exists but differs materially or contains stale navigation/release wording.
- `NEW_TRANSLATION` — Candidate 2 unit has no Spanish release-equivalent.
- `RETIRE` — historical Spanish-only unit must not appear in the new candidate.

A `REUSE_VERIFIED` classification is semantic/text authority only. Final punctuation, typography, pagination, bookmarks and layout remain subject to later candidate QA.
