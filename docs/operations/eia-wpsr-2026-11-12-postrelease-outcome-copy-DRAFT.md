## EIA oil inventories: released Thursday, 12 November

The U.S. Energy Information Administration's Weekly Petroleum Status Report was published on **Thursday, 12 November 2026**, after the Veterans Day schedule adjustment. Its figures describe the **week ending Friday, 6 November**; that data-week ending date is **not** the publication date. [A]

### Confirmed results

- **Commercial U.S. crude oil inventories excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve increased 4.7 million barrels to 414.4 million barrels**. EIA's underlying archive Table 1 records **414.417 million** versus **409.760 million** a week earlier, a change of **4.657 million**, rounded in the published summary. [B, C]
- **Total motor gasoline stocks rose 1.8 million barrels to 210.8 million** (underlying Table 1: 210.757 versus 208.958). [B, C]
- **Distillate fuel oil stocks rose 4.1 million barrels to 103.3 million** (underlying Table 1: 103.330 versus 99.259). [B, C]
- **Refineries processed approximately 17.0 million barrels of crude per day, 322,000 barrels/day more than the prior week, at 95.2% capacity utilization**. [B]
