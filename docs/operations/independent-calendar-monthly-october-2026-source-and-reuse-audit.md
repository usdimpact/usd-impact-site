September labor and consumer prices and Q3 growth/compensation were published in October. The article must not claim complete month-wide coverage, market correlations, DXY/WTI/Gold/BTC/index returns, any original USD Impact Score or an observed USD move. ## Issuer publication and reuse policy cross-check (not a legal approval)

- **BLS:** https://www.bls.gov/bls/linksite.htm states BLS-published material is generally public domain, with previously copyrighted illustrations and photographs excepted; attribution is requested. BLS emblems are protected. This Draft contains original prose and hyperlinks, no copied source pictures or emblems.
- **BEA:** https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/147 says its web-published information is public domain unless stated otherwise and appreciates source attribution. https://www.bea.gov/about/policies-and-information/linking permits ordinary linking and warns against implying endorsement. This Draft links directly, identifies BEA and uses no BEA logo.
- **Federal Reserve Board:** https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm states website information is generally public domain unless otherwise indicated, requires recognition of third-party rights and says seals/logos require permission. Its separate non-endorsement and hyperlinking restrictions still apply; this Draft links to the dated Board statement and does not embed a page or seal.

**Limit:** Favorable public-domain and official-link notices are evidence of their public policies, **not** a separately executed permission receipt for every special element, all non-U.S. jurisdictions, every distribution format, third-party assets, proprietary financial feeds or the original Score. The public page uses no reused charts, embedded issuer HTML/PDF, vendor prices, logos or photographs. Keep product-specific rights/legal/editorial approval separate. Do not broaden this review to newsletter, audio/video, downloadable PDF, translation, historical source scraping or market data without specific clearance.
