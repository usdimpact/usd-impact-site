## What the public policies actually support

- **Federal Reserve Board:** [Board copyright/trademark disclaimer](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm) says Board-site information is generally public domain unless indicated otherwise, requests Board citation, allows direct hyperlinks, and excludes protected third-party graphics and use of Board seals/official marks. **Policy evidence, not a blanket commercial ingestion or archival grant.**
- **BEA:** [BEA copyright FAQ](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/147) says BEA-site material is generally public domain unless stated otherwise and appreciates a BEA source citation. Exact source item, non-BEA content and user-facing representation still need review.
- **BLS:** [BLS linking/copyright notice](https://www.bls.gov/bls/linksite.htm) permits reuse of public-domain BLS material with BLS citation, while restricting previously copyrighted images/illustrations and emblem use. Do not treat its release calendar as an outcome or imply BLS endorsement.
- **EIA (future optional only):** [EIA copyright/reuse](https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php) permits redistribution of many EIA-origin materials with date-specific acknowledgment, but excludes protected external content, licensed imagery and the EIA logo. **Not currently an eligible independent editorial claim.**
- **Federal caution:** [USAGov copyright guidance](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright) notes that not everything on a federal domain is an agency work and trademark/privacy/third-party/cross-border issues can remain.

These references are relevant to link-out and accurate issuer attribution. They are **not** S&P, Cboe, CME, ICE, Yahoo, FRED or other commercial market-data rights, nor permission to copy an old Score dataset. The proposed editor-only work can reuse and cite the project's *own* previously reviewed Daily prose, but a final publication still needs source-specific review.

## Release-date, vintage and scope constraints

- **Fed:** Oct 26–27 schedule previews do **not** amount to an October 28 decision. The Oct 28 decision is the separately dated statement.
- **BEA:** Oct 29 GDP is Q3 **seasonally adjusted annualized** real growth; the distinct Oct 29 Personal Income and Outlays release reports **September** PCE monthly/y/y measures. No same-day price reaction follows from those releases alone.
- **BLS:** Oct 30 Q3 ECI is **0.8% q/q SA** vs **0.9% in Q2**, plus **3.4% y/y NSA**. The Oct 30 Daily's original `generatedAt=09:34 UTC` preceded the BLS **12:30 UTC** release. Use the documented [post-release editorial correction PR #1009](https://github.com/usdimpact/usd-impact-site/pull/1009) and a reviewed claim receipt; never fabricate pre-release knowledge.
- **No licensed market data:** no dollar-price, DXY, SPX, VIX, WTI futures, gold futures, BTC, Score z-scores/weights/contributions or returns may be copied from the original Score or commercial feeds. Government releases may be discussed as *conditional transmission channels*, not as measured outcomes or market responses.
- **No future claims:** references to a future scheduled November event are calendars only until the issuing agency actually publishes it; later corrections must be as-of dated.
- **No direct-source automatic ingestion yet:** link-out and existing reviewed Daily text are the present evidence. Separate approval required before agency HTML/XML/API GET, caching, screenshots, charts, automated extraction, object storage, cross-border duplication, or new third-party content.
