## Romanian and European law context — checked for this narrow original-facts use

- [Romania's current consolidated Copyright Law No. 8/1996, Article 9](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/252971) excludes **ideas, news/press information and simple facts/data** from copyright protection (Article 9(a), (e), (f)). For a Romania-operated publisher that writes its own prose around five public federal-release facts, this is a material favorable distinction from reproducing an author's protected expression. It is not by itself a decision about every aspect of non-Romanian works or all foreign reader jurisdictions.
- [WIPO Lex (Romanian Law No. 8/1996, amended through Law No. 69/2022)](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/law/neighboring_rights_romania) corroborates the Romanian Art. 9 exclusions; use the official Romanian legislative portal as the primary reference.
- [EU Database Directive 96/9/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1996/9/oj) separately governs extraction/reuse of protected databases. The candidate links to five standalone government news releases, summarizes a small set of headline facts, does **not** download or systematically harvest an agency/market-data database and does not reproduce a substantial selection of a protected commercial database. Retain this boundary; any future archive, API, price feed, compiled dataset or wholesale scraping needs a new assessment.
- [USAGov's 2026 government materials guidance](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright) expressly warns that U.S. government works may receive copyright protection outside the United States; protected third-party works, agency logos, trademark/endorsement and privacy/publicity rights need distinct analysis. Therefore *U.S. federal public domain* is not a blanket global grant to republish whole materials.
- A plain hyperlink and an independently expressed historical fact are materially different from copying the agency's expressive release. The draft contains issuer credit and avoids suggesting endorsement or agency partnership.

**Assessment for the *narrow draft*:** the facts/original-prose/link-only design substantially avoids the largest permissions problem and does not inherently require the original Score's S&P DJI or any separate financial-vendor data license. It supports proceeding with a *review candidate*, but cannot independently certify all trademark, cross-border, database, archival, defamation, privacy or advertising-law issues for a deployed public commercial website. The owner has now accepted the narrowly defined facts/original-prose/issuer-links scope for this publisher after considering the recorded Romania/EU/international caveats, in the [exact-version scope receipt](release-candidate-2026-10-30.owner-editorial-and-narrow-use-pass.draft.json). This records a publisher use decision, **not** an independently qualified opinion on every foreign legal jurisdiction or third-party exception before Production.

## Remaining localized publication concerns

1. **Attribution/no endorsement.** Use plain-text issuer names and direct links; never present the agencies as sponsors, partners or certifiers. Avoid logos/seals, official screenshots, embedded source pages and rehosted press-release graphics.
2. **Web operations/privacy/cookies.** The draft page has no analytics, forms, external assets or tracking. A future public integration into the main website may use the site's global scripts, cookie consent and legal/privacy notices; verify those against deployed behavior separately. This assessment is not a privacy/cookie compliance audit.
3. **Retention.** Storing USD Impact's original report and immutable internal provenance is not equivalent to rehosting protected third-party pages. The current candidate stores only original content and URLs, not an external agency snapshot. Any later rehosting of source PDFs, charts or automated provider data needs a distinct rights and retention decision.
4. **International distribution.** Romanian law and U.S. agency policies are favorable inputs but not proof that every jurisdiction treats all U.S. government works identically. Assess the actual intended distribution and relevant EU and LATAM exposures, without assuming an international marketing claim is licensed by U.S. public-domain rules.
5. **Publication time.** The published article may truthfully cite each issuer-stated release/embargo time without asserting independently observed HTTP posting instants. The actual date of *USD Impact's* publication must be recorded only when publication occurs, never backdated to 30 October or the weekly coverage dates.
6. **Future editing.** Adding market-price claims, vendor-derived numbers, original Score calculations, agency images or additional outlets invalidates this narrow review and requires new sourcing and legal scope.
