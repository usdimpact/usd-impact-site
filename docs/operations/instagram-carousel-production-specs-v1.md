# Instagram Carousel Production Specs v1

Status: publishable-quality base specifications. No Instagram/Meta connection, scheduling, or posting authorization.

## Global visual contract

- Format: 1080x1350 portrait carousel.
- Brand: Midnight #020A14, Navy #071A33, Gold #C9A35B, White #FFFFFF.
- Display: Playfair Display Variable bold.
- Body/UI: Inter Variable.
- Horizontal USD Impact logo in a fixed safe-area position.
- One concept per carousel.
- 5–7 slides preferred.
- Large mobile-readable typography.
- No fake terminals, hype imagery, price targets, trading signals, or personalized recommendations.
- Final slide includes one CTA and `Educational only.`
- Source note required when definitions or diagrams depend on external primary material.
- Evergreen carousels must contain no mutable market statistics.

## CAR-002 — DXY: Signal vs System

Class: EVERGREEN  
CTA: Framework

**Slide 1**  
DXY: SIGNAL vs SYSTEM

**Slide 2**  
WHAT DXY DOES  
It provides a compact signal for the U.S. dollar against a specific basket of major currencies.

**Slide 3**  
WHAT DXY DOES NOT DO  
It does not measure every global dollar funding relationship.

**Slide 4**  
THE BROADER SYSTEM  
Dollar loans, deposits, bonds, FX swaps, repo, collateral and cross-border contracts can matter outside the DXY basket.

**Slide 5 — Common mistake**  
DXY UP ≠ AUTOMATIC FUNDING STRESS  
Exchange-rate pressure and funding stress can overlap, but they are not the same thing.

**Slide 6 — Takeaway**  
Use DXY for the dollar signal.  
Then check the funding system around it.  
Framework → USD Impact  
Educational only.

## CAR-003 — Nominal Yield vs Real Yield

Class: EVERGREEN  
CTA: Learn

**Slide 1**  
NOMINAL YIELD vs REAL YIELD

**Slide 2**  
NOMINAL YIELD  
The stated yield before adjusting for expected inflation.

**Slide 3**  
REAL YIELD  
Conceptually: nominal yield minus expected inflation.

**Slide 4**  
WHY THE DISTINCTION MATTERS  
Two nominal-yield moves can imply different real-rate pressure if inflation expectations move differently.

**Slide 5**  
WHY MARKETS CARE  
Real yields help frame inflation-adjusted financing and discount-rate pressure.

**Slide 6 — Common mistake**  
HIGHER NOMINAL YIELDS ≠ ALWAYS HIGHER REAL YIELDS

**Slide 7 — Takeaway**  
Check the real-rate channel, not only the headline yield.  
Learn → USD Impact  
Educational only.

## CAR-010 — Fact vs Interpretation vs Forecast

Class: EVERGREEN  
CTA: Start Here

**Slide 1**  
FACT vs INTERPRETATION vs FORECAST

**Slide 2**  
FACT  
An observed statement that can be verified with evidence.

Example: “DXY rose during the session.”

**Slide 3**  
INTERPRETATION  
An explanation of a possible mechanism.

Example: “Dollar strength added pressure to gold.”

**Slide 4**  
FORECAST  
A claim about a future outcome.

Example: “Gold will fall further tomorrow.”

**Slide 5 — Common mistake**  
Do not present an interpretation as if it were a verified fact.

**Slide 6 — Takeaway**  
Verify the fact.  
Label the interpretation.  
Separate the forecast.  
Educational only.
