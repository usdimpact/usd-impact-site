Status: reusable fixture-only production base. No Instagram/Meta publishing connection, scheduling, credential setup, or Production activation.

## Purpose

Provide reusable creative shells for the governed source-to-social workflow without carrying September 2026 market facts forward into a future posting queue.

The source publication remains authoritative. These templates may simplify and visualize verified source fields; they must not create a second factual truth layer.

## Source-field contract

Templates accept only governed source-derived fields such as:

- `source_identifier`;
- `source_date`;
- `source_url_or_repo_ref`;
- verified hook / regime / event / result / evidence fields;
- uncertainty or caveat;
- one governed CTA and destination.

No source means no publishable creative.

## Family-specific rules

### Daily

- Current verified Daily edition only.
- Highest-information move, not necessarily largest percentage move.
- Dollar / real rates / liquidity or funding / selected asset context.
- Candidate becomes stale when the Daily is no longer current relevant context.

### Weekly Score

- Completed governed week only.
- Dollar, real rates, liquidity/stress, cross-asset context, upcoming catalyst.
- Score is educational regime context, not a forecast or trading signal.

### Catalyst Ahead

- Event identity, schedule and primary source must be verified.
- Explain possible transmission channels conditionally.
- Do not predict the outcome.

### Catalyst Result

- Actual result and market reaction must be verified before creative use.
- Include source timestamp, USD/rates/asset reaction and whether the first move held.
- Fail closed if outcome or reaction cannot be verified.

### Report

- Published or explicitly approved USD Impact report only.
- Question, context, evidence, mechanism, cross-asset implications, risks/uncertainty.
- Derivative social content must remain understandable without omitted context.
