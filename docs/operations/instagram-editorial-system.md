# USD Impact Instagram Editorial System v1

Status: internal implementation specification.

Purpose: define a repeatable, compliance-safe Instagram publishing system that reuses USD Impact's existing Daily, Weekly Score, catalyst, report, framework, and Library content without turning the channel into a trading-signal feed.

## 1. Channel proposition

Instagram is the short-form distribution layer of USD Impact.

Core promise: help viewers understand why markets are moving by separating:

1. U.S. dollar direction;
2. U.S. real-rate pressure;
3. liquidity and funding stress;
4. asset-specific forces.

The channel is educational and informational only. It does not provide personalized recommendations, buy/sell instructions, trading signals, guaranteed outcomes, or promises of performance.

## 2. Audience

Primary:

- new and intermediate investors;
- market observers who follow more than one asset;
- learners trying to understand macro transmission rather than memorize headlines;
- readers who may later use the public framework, Daily USD Impact, Weekly Score, reports, free Library sample, or Library Pass.

## 4. Content families

### A. USD Impact Today

Purpose: current-market discovery.

Inputs: Daily USD Impact and verified same-day market context.

Outputs:

- 20–35 second Reel;
- optional Daily Snapshot card;
- Story sequence;
- optional framework quiz.

Structure:

1. hook: what changed;
2. evidence: dollar / real rates / liquidity;
3. cross-asset implication;
4. what to watch;
5. source / CTA.

### B. Weekly Score

Purpose: flagship recurring systematic view.

Outputs:

- 45–60 second Reel;
- 5–7 slide carousel;
- Story recap or quiz.

Structure:

1. current regime;
2. USD direction;
3. real-rate direction;
4. liquidity/stress state;
5. selected cross-asset implications;
6. uncertainty / caveat;
7. link to complete Weekly Score.

### C. Catalyst Ahead

Purpose: explain why a scheduled event matters before release.

Eligible examples: FOMC, CPI, payrolls, ECB, BoE, major energy data, verified high-impact geopolitical catalysts.

Do not predict the result. Explain transmission channels and what new information would matter.

### D. Catalyst Result

Purpose: explain a verified result after release.

Required fields:

- expected / prior where relevant;
- verified actual result;
- immediate USD response;
- rates response;
- selected cross-asset response;
- uncertainty / whether the first move held.

### E. One Chart / One Lesson

Purpose: teach one relationship at a time.

Examples:

- DXY vs gold;
- real yields vs gold;
- DXY vs EURUSD;
- WTI vs USD;
- BTC vs liquidity;
- HY OAS / VIX and liquidity stress;
- Henry Hub vs TTF;
- Nasdaq vs real yields.

### F. Read the Dollar First

Purpose: evergreen framework education.

Topics:

- the three dials;
- what changed first;
- transmission chains;
- dollar-led vs rate-led vs liquidity-led vs asset-specific moves;
- common analytical mistakes.

### G. Macro Myth

Purpose: correct oversimplified market narratives.

Examples:

- "Gold always rises when the dollar falls";
- "A risk-off day always means a stronger dollar";
- "Bitcoin is only a liquidity trade";
- "Oil is only about supply";
- "One Fed headline explains every asset move."

Use evidence and uncertainty. Do not replace one absolute claim with another.

### H. Test Your Framework

Purpose: interaction and learning.

Formats:

- Story quiz;
- poll;
- true/false;
- "Which force would you investigate first?";
- "What changed first?";
- "Dollar or rates?";
- "Which chart matters?"

Avoid "What happens next?" framing when it implies a trading prediction.

### I. Reports / Deep Dives

Purpose: convert longer USD Impact research into high-value public explainers.

Outputs per major report:

- 60–90 second flagship Reel;
- 5–8 slide carousel;
- 2–3 micro-Reels;
- quiz or chart card.

### J. Library-derived micro-lessons

Purpose: use the 51-film Library as intellectual source material without reproducing the paid product.

For each film identify:

1. core concept;
2. misconception;
3. visual explanation;
4. quiz;
5. practical interpretation question.

Default rule: create new public micro-lessons rather than simply cutting paid films into free excerpts.

## 5. Master format specifications

### Vertical video

Master canvas: 1080 × 1920.

Required:

- voice-over, no presenter by default;
- English captions burned in or delivered as reliable on-screen text;
- large mobile-readable typography;
- safe top/bottom margins for Instagram UI;
- small consistent USD Impact mark;
- real charts/diagrams when useful;
- end-frame source and CTA;
- understandable without audio.

### Feed carousel / card

Preferred: 1080 × 1350 for feed readability.

Supported: 1080 × 1080 where a square derivative is useful.

### Video duration tiers

- Tier A — Daily / result: 15–35 sec
- Tier B — Educational: 30–60 sec
- Tier C — Flagship / report: 60–90 sec

## 9. Compliance and editorial guardrails

Every public post must:

- distinguish verified facts from interpretation;
- use current authoritative sources for time-sensitive claims;
- preserve uncertainty where evidence is mixed;
- avoid personalized investment advice;
- avoid buy/sell/hold instructions;
- avoid implied trading signals;
- avoid guaranteed outcomes or performance claims;
- avoid unsupported forecasts;
- avoid sensationalized financial imagery or get-rich-fast framing;
- label sponsored/affiliate relationships when applicable;
- include an educational-only disclosure where context requires it.

Preferred framework question:

> Which force would you investigate first?

Avoid:

> What should you buy now?

## 10. Visual language

Preserve the existing USD Impact presentation style:

- calm;
- evidence-led;
- clean typography;
- restrained motion;
- charts before decorative finance imagery;
- consistent tickers and labels;
- no presenter required;
- no luxury cars, cash stacks, rockets, exaggerated P&L screens, or fear/greed clickbait.

Each format should be recognizable before the viewer reads the account name.

Suggested recurring openers:

- READ THE DOLLAR FIRST
- ONE CHART. ONE LESSON.
- MACRO MYTH
- CATALYST AHEAD
- CATALYST RESULT
- WEEKLY SCORE
- TEST YOUR FRAMEWORK

## 11. Editorial cadence

Default mature cadence:

| Day | Primary | Supporting |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Weekly Score Reel | Score carousel / quiz |
| Tuesday | Daily Impact | Story snapshot |
| Wednesday | One Chart / One Lesson | Quiz |
| Thursday | Daily Impact or educational Reel | Macro Myth |
| Friday | Week in 60 Seconds | Story recap |
| Saturday | Report / Deep Dive | Report carousel |
| Sunday | Evergreen lesson | Upcoming catalysts |

High-impact verified catalysts supersede lower-value scheduled content.

Initial target: 7–10 feed pieces per week after templates and QA are stable. Stories may be more frequent on active market days.

## 12. Content mix

Target mix:

- 70% education / current context;
- 20% framework interaction / brand;
- 10% direct product evaluation or conversion.

The Library Pass should be the logical next level, not the CTA on every post.

## 13. Funnel

## 15. QA gate

No post is publishable until all applicable checks pass.

### Facts

- source verified;
- data/date correct;
- event identity correct;
- asset/ticker labels correct;
- outcome verified before result content.

### Editorial

- one primary idea;
- hook understandable in first seconds;
- jargon defined or avoided;
- fact vs interpretation separated;
- no unsupported certainty.

### Visual

- correct aspect ratio;
- safe margins;
- captions checked;
- charts readable on phone;
- logo/brand correct;
- no clipping or broken transitions.

### Compliance

- educational framing;
- no personalized recommendation;
- no trading instruction;
- no guaranteed result;
- no unsupported forecast;
- disclosure present where required.

### Technical

- thumbnail/cover checked;
- audio intelligible;
- link destination tested;
- caption/copy proofread;
- source/end card present where applicable.

## 16. Measurement

Measure by content family and format, not only by individual post.

Primary:

- Reel completion rate;
- average watch duration;
- saves;
- shares;
- carousel completion;
- Story responses;
- profile visits;
- website clicks;
- Start Here visits;
- report visits;
- free sample visits;
- Library evaluation / conversion where attributable and privacy-compliant.

Secondary:

- follower growth;
- comments;
- likes.

After the first 30 days, review topic × format × hook × duration combinations before increasing cadence.
