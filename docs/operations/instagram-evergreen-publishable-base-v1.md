# Instagram Evergreen Publishable Base v1

Status: production-base candidate; no Instagram/Meta publishing connection or schedule activation.

## Objective

Build a publishable-quality library before launch without tying the library to a specific trading day. Assets should remain useful at an October 2026 launch unless their review class requires refresh.

## Publishability classes

- **EVERGREEN** — concept and script are designed to remain publishable without current-market data. Recheck only product links, brand/compliance, and factual definitions before posting.
- **ROLLING** — durable lesson with an example, score, chart, statistic, policy setting, or current product detail that must be refreshed before posting.
- **EVENT** — high-importance catalyst asset. Publish only while timely and after fresh primary-source verification. Historical versions remain production fixtures, not queued posts.

No asset is automatically published. `publishable-quality` means creative, sourcing, compliance, captions and CTA can pass review; it does not authorize posting.

## Target base

- 24 evergreen/rolling educational Reels
- 12 supporting carousels
- 10 Story/quiz interactions
- 10 completed HOW onboarding videos
- reusable EVENT templates for major catalysts

Editorial target for the eventual feed: approximately 70% evergreen education, 20% platform/how-to and interaction, 10% high-importance event-driven/current context.

## Reusable creative contract

### Reel

- 9:16, 1080×1920.
- Presenter-free unless explicitly approved otherwise.
- USD Impact — Production brand kit.
- One lesson per Reel.
- Target 25–50 seconds for evergreen education; longer only when the concept requires it.
- Hook in first 2 seconds; avoid fear/hype/trader imagery.
- Large burned-in captions plus retained subtitle file.
- One CTA only.
- End card includes `Educational only.`
- No live price/score/statistic unless asset is ROLLING/EVENT and refreshed immediately before approval.

### Carousel

- Cover: one question or concept.
- 3–6 teaching slides.
- One `Common mistake` or `What this does not mean` slide where useful.
- One takeaway.
- One CTA.
- Source note when a factual definition/diagram depends on external material.

### Story / quiz

- Question first.
- One unambiguous educational answer.
- Explanation after response.
- No pseudo-signal language such as `bullish`, `bearish`, `buy`, `sell`, `entry`, `target`, or implied recommendation unless those words are being explicitly defined/criticized in an educational context.

## Source and compliance gate

Before an asset receives `READY-FOR-POSTING-REVIEW`:

1. Durable factual claims are checked against USD Impact governed material and, where appropriate, primary institutional sources.
2. No current statistic is carried forward from a production fixture without refresh.
3. Distinguish observed fact, interpretation and conditional mechanism.
4. Do not state that correlation proves causality.
5. No personalized recommendation, buy/sell instruction, target, guaranteed outcome, expected return or unsupported forecast.
6. Third-party chart/image licensing is confirmed or the visual is redrawn from permitted data/source material.
7. Captions and subtitle file exist.
8. CTA resolves to the intended USD Impact public/member surface.
9. Final status remains a human-reviewed creative state; publishing authorization is separate.

## Launch freshness rule

At launch, classify each finished asset:

- EVERGREEN: verify links/product wording and post-review date; no market-data refresh required if no mutable fact is present.
- ROLLING: replace all dated values/examples with current governed evidence and rerun editorial/compliance review.
- EVENT: generate from the then-current verified event packet; never substitute an old event because a creative slot exists.

The September 2026 Daily/Weekly/FOMC packets remain evidence and template fixtures. They are not launch-queue commitments.
