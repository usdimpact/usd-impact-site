# USD Impact Instagram Pilot Production Pack v1

Status: internal production specification.

Scope: five launch pilots for the approved USD Impact Instagram system. This is creative-production documentation only. It does not publish, authenticate to Instagram/Meta, add credentials, modify Production, or activate automated posting.

## Global production standard

### Master delivery

- Canvas: 1080 x 1920 vertical.
- Delivery target: 30 fps unless a production tool requires another standard.
- Voice: calm international English; measured, analytical, no hype.
- Presenter: none by default.
- Captions: always on; sentence case; 1–2 short lines per frame; mobile-readable.
- Brand mark: small USD Impact mark in a fixed safe-area position.
- Motion: restrained; use purposeful transitions, chart reveals, arrows, highlights, and controlled zooms.
- Music: optional, low-level, non-dramatic; voice and comprehension take priority.
- End frame: source cue + CTA + educational-only language where appropriate.
- Safe-area principle: keep essential text away from top controls, bottom caption/CTA areas, and right-side action controls.

### Visual hierarchy

Each pilot should use this order:

1. one high-contrast hook;
2. one visual idea per scene;
3. evidence before interpretation;
4. a simple transmission mechanism;
5. one takeaway;
6. one CTA.

### Caption style

Captions should not duplicate every visual label. They should carry the spoken narrative while charts, arrows, and labels carry structure.

Avoid:

- all-caps paragraphs;
- more than two competing chart panels at once;
- decorative candlesticks unrelated to the lesson;
- stock footage of traders, cash, skyscrapers, luxury imagery, rockets, or exaggerated profit/loss screens.

### Source treatment

Evergreen lessons may use canonical institutional/reference sources. Time-sensitive pilots require fresh authoritative sources at production time.

Preferred sources by topic include:

- Federal Reserve / FRED for rates, dollar indexes, funding indicators, and official series;
- U.S. Treasury for Treasury market context where needed;
- ICE methodology or other authoritative index documentation for DXY definitions where licensing/display permits;
- official exchange/reference documentation for instrument definitions;
- USD Impact's own public framework pages for internal methodology references.

Do not place third-party chart imagery in the final creative unless its use is permitted. Prefer recreated charts from licensed/approved data or original diagrams.

Pinned-post candidate and channel manifesto.

## Objective

Teach the core USD Impact habit in under 40 seconds: before explaining an asset move with a headline, identify whether the dominant pressure is dollar-led, real-rate-led, liquidity-led, or asset-specific.

## Target duration

32–38 seconds.

## Primary CTA

Learn the framework → Start Here.

## Cover frame

Primary text:

> READ THE DOLLAR FIRST

Secondary text:

> Before the market headline.

Visual:

A restrained transmission-chain motif connecting USD → real rates → liquidity → assets.

Do not use asset-price arrows implying a forecast.

## Final voice-over

> Before you explain a market move with a headline, ask a more useful question: what changed first? Start with the dollar. Then check real rates and liquidity. Only after that, look at what is specific to the asset. Gold, Bitcoin, oil, equities and FX can react to the same headline for very different reasons. Read the driver before you read the story. That is the USD Impact framework.

# Pilot 3 — DXY in 30 Seconds

## Role

Evergreen glossary / One Chart, One Lesson pilot.

## Objective

Explain what DXY is, what it is not, and why USD Impact distinguishes DXY from the broader concept of the dollar.

## Target duration

28–34 seconds.

## Primary CTA

Learn the framework → Start Here.

## Cover frame

> DXY IN 30 SECONDS

Subhead:

> A dollar index — not "the dollar."

## Final voice-over

> DXY is a dollar index, but it is not the entire dollar system. It measures the U.S. dollar against a fixed basket of major currencies, with the euro carrying the largest weight. That makes DXY useful for tracking one important version of dollar direction. But global dollar funding, emerging-market currencies, trade-weighted measures and offshore dollar stress can tell a different story. Use DXY as a signal. Do not mistake it for the whole system.

## Role

## Target duration

## Primary CTA

Explore the framework → Start Here.

## Cover frame

> DOLLAR vs GOLD

Subhead:

> Relationship ≠ rule

Visual:

Two clean unlabeled lines diverging and reconnecting; no implication of current price direction.

## Final voice-over

> A weaker dollar can support gold, and a stronger dollar can pressure it. But that relationship is not a law. Gold also responds to real yields, risk conditions, liquidity, central-bank demand, positioning and its own market structure. So when gold and the dollar diverge, do not force the old rule onto the new evidence. Check the dollar, then real rates, then liquidity, then what is specific to gold. Relationship does not mean certainty.

# Pilot 5 — Repo in Plain English

## Role

Library-derived public micro-lesson.

## Objective

Explain repo as short-term collateralized funding and connect it to dollar liquidity without reproducing a paid film.

## Target duration

42–50 seconds.

## Primary CTA

Explore Read the Dollar First.

## Cover frame

> REPO IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Subhead:

> Short-term funding with collateral.

Visual:

Two counterparties, cash on one side and Treasury collateral on the other.

## Final voice-over

> Repo is one of the financial system's core short-term funding markets. In a typical repo, one party receives cash and provides securities as collateral, with an agreement to reverse the transaction later. Economically, it behaves like a secured short-term loan. Why does it matter? Because dealers, funds and other institutions use repo to finance securities and manage liquidity. When repo funding becomes harder or more expensive, that can be one sign that dollar liquidity is under pressure. It is a funding channel, not a standalone market forecast.
