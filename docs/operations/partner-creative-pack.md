## 1. Two-minute Partner Program overview

USD Impact is an educational macro-finance platform built around one discipline: read the dollar regime before interpreting an individual asset move.

The future Partner Program is designed for carefully selected educators, publishers, creators, newsletters, communities, and professional learning organizations whose audiences want to understand how the dollar, real rates, liquidity, funding, and asset-specific forces interact.

The initial eligible offer is the full-price *Read the Dollar First* Library Pass. It is a one-time purchase that includes:

- the complete Guided Interactive Edition;
- the complete English audiobook;
- the 51-film USD Impact Video Library with verified English captions;
- interactive quizzes, saved progress, and protected reference resources;
- ongoing access to the purchased edition, with no recurring Library Pass subscription fee.

The public product page currently displays a USD 39 launch price and a USD 49 standard price after the approved launch offer ends. Partners must always use the current approved Offer Card and destination page rather than hard-coding a price into evergreen content.

USD Impact is educational. It is not investment advice, a trading-signal service, a forecasting product, a portfolio recommendation, a broker, or a promise of investment performance.

## 3. Partner proposition

### Core proposition

Give your audience a structured way to understand dollar, real-rate, liquidity, and cross-asset transmission - and, once approved and active, earn commission when qualified readers choose the full learning experience.

### Why a partner may care

- Audience value: useful education instead of another prediction or trading call.
- Trust alignment: verified sources, visible methodology, and restrained claims.
- Low publishing friction: approved copy, deep links, disclosure language, and campaign recipes.
- Product clarity: one complete Library Pass rather than fragmented upsells.
- Commercial progression: a starting rate with evidence-based advancement for retained, compliant sales.
- Collaboration options: educational articles, webinars, resource pages, newsletters, and partner-specific learning journeys.

### Who is a strong fit

- macro, rates, FX, commodities, or cross-asset newsletters;
- finance educators, YouTube channels, and podcasts;
- university finance or economics clubs and serious student communities;
- professional finance and evidence-led learning communities;
- independent analysts, consultants, and client-facing educators;
- selected research-tool or fintech communities with compatible educational standards.

### Who is not a fit

- trading-signal, guaranteed-return, or get-rich-fast promoters;
- coupon, cashback, discount-scraping, or deal-leakage networks during beta;
- opaque traffic brokers or sub-affiliate networks;
- promoters using fabricated urgency, undisclosed paid endorsements, or personalized buy/sell calls;
- applicants seeking self-referral, trademark bidding, copied paid content, or unauthorized incentives.

## 13. Claim-control matrix

### Approved without further review when current and accurate

- USD Impact is educational macro-finance content.
- The framework separates dollar direction, real-rate pressure, liquidity stress, and asset-specific forces.
- The Library Pass includes the items in the current product fact sheet.
- A free public learning path and sample are available.
- The Library Pass is a one-time purchase under the current product contract.
- The partner may earn a commission, using the approved disclosure.

### Requires review before publication

- any new product-benefit claim outside the fact sheet;
- comparative or “best” claims;
- statistics about learning, conversion, customer outcomes, or popularity;
- testimonials, ratings, or customer quotations;
- partner-created graphics that alter product, price, or methodology statements;
- translations;
- time-limited offers, bonuses, or scarcity language;
- paid-search, event, institutional, co-branded, or press campaigns;
- any mention of Research Membership or future TradingView access.

### Prohibited

- “know when to buy or sell”;
- “predict gold/Bitcoin/oil/the market”;
- “guaranteed profitable trades” or “expected returns”;
- “beat the market,” “avoid losses,” or “safe profits”;
- personalized investment recommendations;
- false independence or a hidden affiliate relationship;
- fabricated urgency, stock, seat, or price-expiry claims;
- altered or invented prices, discounts, coupons, or bonuses;
- promises of current Research Membership or TradingView access;
- copying or redistributing paid USD Impact content;
- implying regulatory approval, accreditation, or professional certification that does not exist.

A disclaimer does not cure a misleading substantive claim.
