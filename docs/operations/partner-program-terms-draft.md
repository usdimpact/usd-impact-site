# USD Impact Partner Program Terms — Internal Draft

Status: internal draft for legal, provider, privacy, and owner review. Not effective, not published, and not a commercial offer.

Version: 0.1

Provider assumption for this draft: Lemon Squeezy may provide affiliate attribution, referral approval, reporting, KYC/tax onboarding, and payout mechanics if and when the program is activated. This draft does not activate or configure those services.

## 1. Program purpose

The USD Impact Partner Program is an invite/approval-based affiliate program for selected publishers, educators, creators, newsletters, communities, and professional learning organizations.

The program is intended to support accurate educational distribution of USD Impact products and public resources. It is not intended for high-pressure sales, trading-signal promotion, coupon arbitrage, misleading investment claims, or undisclosed endorsements.

Participation is subject to these Terms, the current Partner Creative Pack, the approved Offer Card issued to the partner, applicable provider rules, and any campaign-specific written restrictions.

## 2. Appointment and activation

A partner is not active merely because it has been researched, contacted, conditionally selected, or technically entered into a provider system.

Activation requires all of the following:
- program-wide activation approval has been recorded;
- the partner has been manually approved;
- required identity, tax, payout, and provider onboarding is complete;
- these Terms have been accepted;
- the partner has received an approved Offer Card stating the eligible product(s), commission rate, and any special restrictions;
- approved attribution and destination links have been tested;
- the first campaign or promotional method has been approved where required.

USD Impact may approve, reject, pause, or terminate an application or active partner relationship at its discretion, subject to applicable law and any already-earned obligations.

## 3. Eligible products

Only products expressly listed in the partner's current Offer Card are eligible for commission.

Initial beta eligibility is limited to approved full-price Library Pass purchases unless a later written Offer Card expressly adds another product.

Research Membership is not affiliate-eligible unless separately approved after its own launch, stability, privacy, commercial, and provider gates are complete.

No partner may represent an excluded or inactive product as commissionable.

## 4. Commission rates

Commission rates are issued by Offer Card and are not implied by public documents or prior discussions.

Approved commercial policy currently permits:
- standard opening rate: 15%;
- reviewed higher rates: 20% or 25%;
- exceptional strategic ceiling: 30%.

Thirty percent is a maximum, not a default or automatic tier.

Any higher tier requires a documented review of retained sales, customer quality, refunds, disputes, contribution, compliance, and operational reliability.

USD Impact may change future commission rates or eligibility prospectively by issuing an updated Offer Card or written notice. Changes do not retroactively remove commission already validly earned under the applicable locked transaction rules.

## 5. Commission base and provider fees

Commission is calculated only on the eligible commission base recognized by the approved provider and applicable Offer Card.

Taxes, refunds, chargebacks, disputed amounts, reversed transactions, fraudulent transactions, self-referrals, duplicate transactions, excluded products, or otherwise ineligible amounts do not create commission entitlement.

Provider rules may apply additional affiliate transaction, payout, currency-conversion, tax, or settlement fees. Unless expressly stated otherwise, such provider-side deductions do not increase USD Impact's stated commission rate.

## 6. Attribution

Attribution must use the approved provider-backed mechanism.

Current architecture preference is to use Lemon Squeezy affiliate attribution rather than a custom USD Impact affiliate balance or payout ledger.

Attribution metadata is informational and may never:
- grant, extend, restore, or revoke customer entitlement;
- change product, price, quantity, tax, payment, refund, dispute, or chargeback state;
- override the canonical commerce lifecycle;
- substitute for a verified provider event.

Where website-side attribution requires cookies or another persistent identifier, such tracking may be used only after the applicable privacy/ePrivacy review, notice, consent, retention, and deletion requirements have been approved.

## 7. Attribution window and model

The active attribution window and attribution model are those configured in the approved provider and documented in the partner's Offer Card or onboarding record.

The current planning target is 60–90 days where supported and legally appropriate, but no specific window is promised until activation.

USD Impact may reject attribution that is unsupported, duplicated, manipulated, technically invalid, or contrary to provider records.

## 8. Referral validation and lock period

A referral is not immediately payable merely because a click or order appears in a dashboard.

Commission becomes eligible only after:
- a valid eligible purchase is verified;
- attribution is accepted by the provider and USD Impact;
- fraud, self-referral, duplicate-account, and policy checks pass;
- the applicable refund/dispute/chargeback protection or provider lock period is satisfied;
- the referral has not been rejected or reversed.

Where Lemon Squeezy is used, provider payout and lock mechanics apply in addition to these Terms.

## 9. Refunds, disputes, and chargebacks

Refunded, disputed, charged-back, fraudulent, reversed, or otherwise invalid transactions do not earn final commission.

If commission has not yet been paid, it may be withheld, rejected, reduced, or reversed.

If a provider has already adjusted a payout or referral record, USD Impact may rely on the authoritative provider record unless evidence shows a provider error that must be reconciled.

Attribution status must never alter the customer's valid access rights independently of the canonical payment and entitlement lifecycle.

## 10. No self-referral or circular referral

Partners may not purchase through their own affiliate link or arrange a purchase primarily to generate their own commission.

Prohibited conduct includes:
- self-referral;
- duplicate or controlled accounts;
- circular referral arrangements;
- household or payment-instrument abuse designed to manufacture commission;
- refund/rebuy loops;
- fabricated or incentivized transactions not expressly approved.

Ambiguous cases fail closed pending review.

## 11. No stacking or unauthorized customer incentives

Affiliate commission does not create a customer discount.

Partners may not offer an unauthorized:
- coupon;
- rebate;
- cashback payment;
- gift;
- bonus;
- giveaway;
- discount code;
- price representation;
- purchase incentive.

Affiliate commission may not stack with the separate Member Referral Program or another customer-program benefit on the same purchase unless a later written policy expressly authorizes a specific combined event.

The Member Referral Program remains a separate customer program and is not governed as a cash affiliate commission arrangement.

## 12. Required disclosure

Partners must clearly and prominently disclose their compensated relationship when applicable.

Baseline disclosure:

> I may earn a commission if you purchase USD Impact through this link.

The disclosure must be proximate to the recommendation or link and adapted as necessary to the applicable jurisdiction, platform, and medium without obscuring the commercial relationship.

Partners are responsible for complying with applicable advertising, endorsement, consumer-protection, and platform disclosure rules.

## 13. Permitted claims

Partners may accurately describe USD Impact using the current approved Partner Creative Pack, product page, Offer Card, and other written materials supplied or approved by USD Impact.

USD Impact should be presented as educational macro-finance content and tools for learning how the dollar, rates, liquidity, funding, and cross-asset relationships interact.

Partners may add original commentary, but materially new product claims may require review.

## 14. Prohibited claims

Partners must not present USD Impact as:
- investment advice;
- personalized financial advice;
- a trading-signal service;
- a prediction or forecasting service;
- a portfolio recommendation;
- a broker, exchange, execution venue, custody service, or crypto product;
- a guaranteed or expected-profit system;
- a method that tells users when to buy or sell financial instruments;
- a method that guarantees beating the market or avoiding losses.

Prohibited language includes materially equivalent claims such as:
- "know when to buy Bitcoin";
- "predict gold";
- "guaranteed profitable trades";
- "trading signals";
- "risk-free returns".

## 15. Brand and creative use

Partners may use only approved USD Impact names, logos, screenshots, product descriptions, prices, claims, and creative assets.

Partners may not:
- alter a logo in a misleading way;
- imply ownership of USD Impact intellectual property;
- imply endorsement of the partner's independent investment views;
- publish outdated pricing or product availability after notice of a change;
- copy or redistribute paid USD Impact content.

Evergreen content should link to the live approved destination instead of hard-coding temporary pricing where practical.

## 16. Paid search and trademark use

Paid bidding on USD Impact trademarks, brand names, misspellings, domain variants, or confusingly similar terms is prohibited unless expressly approved in writing.

Partners may not impersonate USD Impact, create misleading domains or social handles, or cause an advertisement to appear as if it were an official USD Impact advertisement without authorization.

## 17. Email, messaging, and direct marketing

Partners using email, direct messages, SMS, messaging apps, or similar channels must comply with all applicable consent, identification, unsubscribe, suppression, privacy, and anti-spam requirements.

USD Impact may prohibit a channel or require retained evidence of consent and compliance.

Purchased lists, scraped contacts, hidden sender identity, or repeated messaging after an opt-out are prohibited.

## 18. Coupon, cashback, and deal sites

Coupon, cashback, discount-scraping, deal-leakage, and similar sites are excluded from the initial beta unless separately approved in writing.

Unauthorized publishing of a rate, coupon, discount, or leaked checkout mechanic is grounds for suspension or termination.

## 19. Sub-affiliates and traffic sources

Sub-affiliate networks require explicit written approval.

Partners must disclose material traffic sources on request and may not use opaque, misleading, fraudulent, incentivized, bot, click-farm, malware, adware, cookie-stuffing, forced-redirect, or similar traffic.

USD Impact may suspend attribution while traffic quality is reviewed.

## 20. Intellectual property

USD Impact retains all rights in its trademarks, logos, content, software, research, videos, audio, graphics, books, reports, and other intellectual property.

Participation grants only the limited, revocable right to use approved partner materials for authorized promotion during the active partner relationship.

No ownership or broader license is transferred.

## 21. Confidential and non-public materials

Internal program documents, unpublished product plans, non-public pricing, partner-specific terms, unreleased research, credentials, security information, customer data, and other designated confidential information may not be disclosed or used outside the approved purpose.

## 22. Customer and personal data

Partners must not receive or collect USD Impact customer personal data merely because they generated a referral.

USD Impact should use stable non-personal affiliate identifiers for reconciliation where supported.

Partners may not attempt to identify customers from referral analytics, combine USD Impact referral data with unrelated personal datasets in a manner not authorized by law or agreement, or use customer purchase information for independent marketing.

## 23. Provider, tax, and payout requirements

Where Lemon Squeezy or another approved affiliate provider performs identity, KYC, tax, payout, or payment functions, the partner must complete those provider requirements.

USD Impact does not guarantee acceptance by the provider, payout availability in every jurisdiction, settlement timing beyond provider rules, or specific tax treatment.

Partners remain responsible for their own tax, reporting, licensing, and business obligations unless applicable law requires otherwise.

## 24. Reporting and audit

USD Impact may review:
- referred visits;
- referral and order records;
- approved and rejected referrals;
- refunds, disputes, and chargebacks;
- traffic quality;
- disclosures and published claims;
- campaign URLs or retained copies;
- compliance incidents;
- customer-concentration and partner-concentration risk;
- provider reconciliation.

Partners must reasonably cooperate with a compliance or reconciliation review.

## 25. Suspension

USD Impact may pause a partner or the entire program for reasons including:
- misleading claims;
- hidden or missing disclosure;
- fraud or suspicious traffic;
- self-referral;
- coupon leakage;
- unauthorized paid search;
- sub-affiliate violations;
- abnormal refunds or disputes;
- provider reconciliation gaps;
- privacy or legal concerns;
- negative economics;
- security or operational risk.

Suspension stops new commission eligibility to the extent permitted by provider rules while preserving authoritative historical records and valid already-earned obligations.

## 26. Termination

Either party may terminate future participation subject to any agreed notice requirements.

USD Impact may terminate immediately for fraud, material misrepresentation, serious compliance breaches, misuse of intellectual property, security abuse, or conduct that creates material legal, customer, or reputational risk.

Termination does not erase authoritative historical order, refund, dispute, or payout records.

## 27. Changes to the program

USD Impact may change future product eligibility, creative rules, attribution settings, campaign restrictions, or commercial terms prospectively.

Material changes affecting an active partner should be communicated through the approved contact method and reflected in an updated Offer Card, Terms version, or provider configuration as appropriate.

## 28. No employment, agency, or investment relationship

A partner is an independent participant and is not an employee, agent, broker, fiduciary, investment adviser, or legal representative of USD Impact.

A partner may not bind USD Impact or make commitments on its behalf.

## 29. No guarantee of revenue

USD Impact does not guarantee traffic, conversions, commissions, sales volume, program duration, or income.

Participation should not be represented as an investment opportunity, employment opportunity, or guaranteed-income arrangement.

## 30. Program records and precedence

For an active partner, the following records should be retained:
- accepted Terms version;
- approved Offer Card;
- partner identity and provider ID;
- approved channels/geographies/languages;
- campaign approvals or restrictions;
- disclosure acknowledgment;
- provider referral and payout records;
- compliance actions;
- suspension/termination records where applicable.

In case of conflict, applicable law and the signed/accepted commercial agreement govern, followed by the active Offer Card and then operational creative guidance, unless the agreement expressly establishes another order.

## 32. Provider-specific schedule — Lemon Squeezy

If Lemon Squeezy is the active affiliate provider:
- affiliate applications and acceptance may be administered through Lemon Squeezy;
- commission/product eligibility/tracking-window settings must match the approved Offer Card;
- Lemon Squeezy referral/order records are authoritative provider evidence for provider-managed attribution;
- Lemon Squeezy payout and lock mechanics apply;
- affiliate IDs and referral amounts may be used for reconciliation and analytics;
- no custom affiliate payout ledger should be introduced without a separate architecture review;
- installation of Lemon Squeezy website-side affiliate tracking must not occur until privacy/ePrivacy requirements are approved;
- provider behavior must be verified in controlled test mode before Production activation.

## 33. Activation checklist

These Terms must remain internal and non-effective until all program-wide gates are complete:

- [ ] Library Pass stability gate completed.
- [ ] Provider-specific attribution and reconciliation tested end to end.
- [ ] Refund/dispute/chargeback reversal behavior proven.
- [ ] Privacy/ePrivacy review completed for tracking.
- [ ] Privacy Notice/cookie disclosures and consent behavior updated where required.
- [ ] Final legal review completed.
- [ ] Legal entity/governing-law/notice section completed.
- [ ] Partner Creative Pack approved and versioned.
- [ ] Application/review workflow ready.
- [ ] First beta partners manually approved.
- [ ] Explicit owner activation approval recorded.

Until then, these Terms do not authorize:
- public partner enrollment;
- live tracking;
- commissions;
- payouts;
- customer discounts;
- Member Referral rewards;
- Research Membership affiliate eligibility;
- provider configuration;
- checkout changes.
