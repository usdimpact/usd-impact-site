# USD Impact Partner Recruitment and Onboarding Playbook

Status: internal English draft. Recruitment, partner approval, tracking, commissions, enrollment, and promotional use remain inactive.

This playbook operationalizes the future invite-only Partner Program without opening public enrollment or changing commerce. It should be used only after Partner Terms, provider behavior, privacy controls, economics, and activation have been separately approved.

## 1. Founding beta objective

Build a small cohort of approximately 10-20 credible partners who can introduce *Read the Dollar First* to audiences already interested in macro, markets, and evidence-led learning.

Success is not the number of affiliate signups. Success is retained, compliant customer acquisition that produces positive net contribution after commission, provider fees, refunds, chargebacks, and support cost.

## 3. 100-point partner score

| Dimension | Weight | High-score evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience/problem fit | 25 | Audience explicitly follows macro, rates, USD, cross-asset education, or adjacent serious learning |
| Trust and compliance fit | 20 | Clear sourcing, restrained language, visible sponsorship disclosures, no hype or deceptive urgency |
| Commercial intent | 15 | Audience buys books, courses, memberships, research, or serious educational products |
| Engagement quality | 10 | Meaningful comments, replies, attendance, reading, or community participation rather than raw followers |
| Distribution quality | 10 | Searchable evergreen content, newsletter, podcast/video archive, resource library, or active community |
| Conversion mechanics | 10 | Can use deep links, educational CTAs, resource pages, webinars, or measured newsletter placements |
| Co-marketing potential | 5 | Credible guest education, webinar, research explainer, or institutional collaboration |
| Operational reliability | 5 | Accurate communication, deadlines, recordkeeping, disclosure discipline, and transparent traffic sources |

Decision rules:

- 75-100: eligible for beta approval review;
- 60-74: manual review or one bounded non-commercial content test;
- below 60: defer;
- any serious fraud, disclosure, financial-claim, content-theft, or traffic-source red flag: reject or suspend regardless of score.

## 7. Partner discovery call structure

Target length: 20-25 minutes.

1. Audience: Who do they serve, and what recurring macro-learning problem does the audience have?
2. Trust: How do they source content, distinguish fact from interpretation, and disclose sponsorships?
3. Distribution: Which newsletter, video, podcast, resource, event, or community placements are realistic?
4. Fit: Which free USD Impact lesson genuinely matches their audience?
5. Boundaries: Confirm no signals, personalized advice, performance promises, self-referral, unauthorized discounting, coupon leakage, or sub-affiliates.
6. Pilot: Choose one small campaign, one destination, and one primary success metric.
7. Operations: Confirm owner, timing, approval process, reporting, and recordkeeping.
8. Next step: approve, request evidence, propose content-only pilot, defer, or reject.

Do not negotiate a live rate, customer discount, tracking method, or launch date before the relevant commercial approvals exist.

## 13. Dormant Member Referral promotion concept - not for publication

The Member Referral Program is separate from the Partner Program and remains unavailable until Research Membership is active, stable, provider-approved, tested, and separately authorized.

Its strategic advantage is peer relevance: a satisfied Research member is more likely than a broad promoter to know colleagues or friends with a similar interest in structured dollar, rates, liquidity, and cross-asset research. Promotion should emphasize shared learning value, never pressure, income, or investment outcomes.

### Future value proposition

> Invite someone who learns markets the way you do. A qualifying new Research client receives 50% off the first annual term, and your successful qualifying introductions move you toward 3, 6, or 12 months of Research access.

At the currently approved ordinary annual reference of USD 290, the new client's first annual payment would be USD 145 before applicable tax. This arithmetic example must be revalidated against the active price immediately before any future release.

### Future reward ladder

| Distinct qualifying paid referrals | Referring member's cumulative free Research access | New client's benefit |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 3 months total | 50% off first annual Research term only |
| 3 | 6 months total | 50% off first annual Research term only |

The referring member receives free Research months only - not 50% off an annual plan, cash, rebate, gift card, or transferable value. The new-client discount and member months are the two intended benefits of one customer-referral event. No affiliate commission or other checkout discount may stack with them.

## 14. Beta measurement and stop rules

Track by partner and campaign:

- approved promotion rate;
- qualified referred visits;
- public-resource and sample engagement;
- checkout starts only when checkout is independently available;
- verified purchases;
- retained purchases after the lock period;
- refunds, disputes, chargebacks, and fraud reviews;
- provider and affiliate fees;
- commission approved, held, voided, or paid;
- net contribution;
- support burden;
- compliance incidents;
- customer concentration and partner concentration.

Pause new recruitment or an individual partner when evidence shows misleading claims, hidden disclosures, suspicious traffic or accounts, self-referral, coupon leakage, rising refunds/disputes, negative contribution, provider reconciliation gaps, or inability to suspend safely.

## 15. Controlled rollout

1. Finalize commercial basis and legal/provider/privacy terms.
2. Prove provider attribution, hold, refund, dispute, chargeback, and reconciliation behavior in the permitted test environment.
3. Release the versioned Creative Pack and application/review record.
4. Research 20-30 qualified prospects and select approximately 10-20 for manual review.
5. Start personalized outreach only after recruitment approval.
6. Onboard the first small wave and run sample-first campaigns.
7. Review contribution and compliance before adding partners or increasing rates.
8. Keep Research Membership affiliate economics and the Member Referral Program dormant until their separate gates pass.

No step in this playbook authorizes Production activation.
