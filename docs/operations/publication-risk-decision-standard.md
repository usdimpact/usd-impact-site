# USD Impact — publication and release risk decision standard

**Version:** 2026-10-31, initial, **reviewed as decision-support only; not legal advice or authorization.** 
**Source record:** [Macro Weekly remediation Issue #1031](https://github.com/usdimpact/usd-impact-site/issues/1031) and the [scope-limited case register](independent-macro-release/first-weekly-remediation-matrix-2026-10-31.draft.json). **Qualified-review upgrade:** [Issue #1030](https://github.com/usdimpact/usd-impact-site/issues/1030).

## Standing rules for EVERY subsequent USD Impact editorial / publication decision

1. **First identify scope, version and rights.** Write the exact repo, owner, article, language, edition, data/media vendor, source URLs, content Git blobs, consent environment, destination project and intended distribution. A PASS for one page, language, provider or presentation **never transfers** to another. Internal exceptions must be explicit, one-object and time-limited where specified. No old Score or paid data grants by inference.
2. **Evidence integrity.** Separate five categories in every answer/issue: **observed verified**, **owner-reported PASS**, **pending independent evidence**, **confirmed defect**, **future-only control**. A green CI, owner UI approval, unobserved browser HAR, source-policy page or internal risk acceptance must never be relabeled qualified legal/privacy certification. Never invent a page's initial publication timestamp, issuer HTTP posting time, financial-market price movement, source entitlement, test result or signed decision.
3. **Copyright and data reuse.** Prefer original factual narrative and direct issuer links. Romania Law 8/1996 Art. 9 excludes simple facts/data; U.S. Federal Reserve, BEA and BLS generally permit reuse of their own information with source credit, but exceptions for third-party expression, agency marks and foreign-law protections remain. Permission to link or report a fact is not permission to copy full articles, third-party charts/photos, seals, providers' proprietary feeds or translated expression. Trigger an actual rights/contract check on scope expansion. If an applicable restriction is established, STOP regardless of business-risk acceptance.
4. **Economic editorial and financial conduct.** Preserve factual sources, timestamps and distinct measurement units. Do not infer price responses or market direction without licensed verified observations. Distinguish conditional macroeconomic transmission from trading advice; review the substance under ESMA MAR/MiFID where implicit trade strategies, asset targets, positions, conflicts, affiliate links or personalized instructions are added. Disclaimers alone cannot rescue misleading substantive recommendations.
5. **AI transparency (applicable from Aug 2, 2026 in relevant EU contexts).** For public-interest AI-generated/manipulated text (economic/financial material can qualify), determine if EU AI Act Article 50(4) applies. Record meaningful **human review or editorial control over substance**, source verification, and identifiable natural/legal **editorial responsibility** when claiming the human-review exception. Superficial spellchecking is insufficient. If exception is not satisfied, assess clear lawful disclosure; do not invent that review occurred or that a label is never required. The historical owner editorial PASS is evidence, not automatic legal proof of all conditions.
6. **Privacy and consent.** No optional analytics loaded or transmitted before the relevant valid opt-in under the site policy. Rejection must not deny core content; withdrawal must stop future optional collection and clean up relevant cookies where technically feasible. Respect existing owner functional PASS but distinguish it from independently captured browser traffic/storage traces and supplier processing/transfer obligations. If a verifiable leak or inconsistent notice appears, stop public activation and fix in a separately reviewed privacy change. Do not request secrets or share cookie values/private HAR headers.
7. **Operator, legal and government attribution.** Verify exact final rendered operator KELA LEADS S.R.L., support contact, Terms/Privacy links, educational/non-advisory disclosure, issuer credits and no implied Fed/BEA/BLS endorsement or unauthorized logos. Record real human editorial responsibility/AI provenance when required. Confirm final page accessibility and mobile behavior.
8. **Release safety and governance.** Pre-publication draft routes remain fail-closed; source and layout receipts must match exact Git objects. Final status copy needs new exact-content owner review and true `published`/Draft labels. For public activation: obtain fresh explicit approval of exact PR/commit, public route, merge, Production project, indexing, navigation and syndication **separately**; test exact date whitelist and adjacent 404s, 6/6 checks, actual runtime-parity preview, 200 response and known-good rollback. Record publication UTC **after** verified public availability; never count archival or Score Weeklies as independent Monthlies.
9. **Stop vs improvement.** A real legal prohibition, third-party license failure, unauthorized analytics collection, unreviewed trading recommendation, source inaccuracy, stale SHA, route leak or unauthorized Production/indexing is a **STOP**. A missing qualified opinion or missing archived HAR is **an evidence gap**, not proof of a violation. A code defense-in-depth opportunity is **a potential improvement**, not a confirmed defect. State the exact missing evidence and next party/decision.
10. **Upgrade rule.** Temporary owner-led internal review Option B is approved **only** for the October 26–30, 2026 English Macro Weekly; it does not waive legal rights or automatically approve other site work. Prefer qualified, affordable independent advice when available; [Issue #1030](https://github.com/usdimpact/usd-impact-site/issues/1030) and existing weekly conditional automation track upgrades. No automatic spending, changing policy, granting rights, merging or deploying.
